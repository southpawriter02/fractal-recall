# Witness Mark Protocols — Documentation Standards for Consent Substitution & Operational Attestation

**Document ID:** JRS-SOP-WITNESS-MARKS-v1.0
**Layer:** 2 (The Echoes of Forging)
**Classification:** Protocol • Field Documentation • Evidence
**Authored by:** Scriptorium of the Jötun-Readers

---

## 1.0 Purpose and Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) codifies **witness mark protocols** for field operations, mystic interventions, and custody events where prior consent cannot be obtained or where neutral attestation is required. Witness marks serve three primary functions:

1. **Consent Substitution**: Documenting interventions performed without prior consent in crisis situations
2. **Neutral Attestation**: Providing third-party verification of custody transfers, evidence handling, and contract execution
3. **Operational Logging**: Recording presence, actions, and outcomes for accountability and safety

Witness marks are required whenever mystic practice touches individuals without advance consent, when custody of hazardous materials or evidence changes hands, or when operational transparency demands independent verification.

**Scope**: All field teams, clinical practitioners, custody handlers, and contract executors operating under Jötun-Reader governance or interfacing with Rangers Guild, Grey Blades, Dvergr Guilds, or Bone-Setter networks.

## 3.0 Types of Witness Marks

### 3.1 Consent Substitution Marks

**Context**: Mystic interventions performed on individuals during crisis when prior consent cannot be obtained.

**Required Documentation**:
- Practitioner identity and credentials
- Subject identity (if known) or description
- Intervention type and rationale
- Timestamp (start and completion)
- Witness identity (minimum one independent observer)
- Outcome and any adverse effects
- Post-intervention briefing status

**Applications**:
- Jötun-Reader causal pathology and threadwork
- Norn-Spinner probability trims
- Örlög-Bound fate adjustments
- Blót-Priest sanguine rites
- Rúnasmiðr inscription containment
- Any mystic practice affecting individuals without advance consent

**Ethics Requirement**: Practitioners must attempt post-intervention briefing when subject has recovered capacity. Consent substitution marks do not authorize ongoing work—only document emergency interventions.

### 3.2 Neutral Attestation Marks (Witness Pairs)

**Context**: Custody transfers, evidence handling, contract execution, and plaza incidents requiring independent verification.

**Required Documentation**:
- Witness Pair IDs (two independent, creed-neutral observers)
- Event type and DocketID (if applicable)
- Parties involved
- Exhibits, materials, or evidence items
- Venue and jurisdiction
- Timestamp and duration
- Window compliance (if attenuation windows apply)
- Seal verification (for custody items)
- Outcome disposition

**Applications**:
- Rangers Guild custody events and corridor arrivals
- Grey Blades evidence registration and contract enforcement
- Dvergr hall proceedings and vault transfers
- Golden Serpent reagent custody handoffs
- Any H≥3 hazard custody transfer

**Neutrality Standards**: Witness pairs must be creed-neutral or represent opposing interests. Self-dealing (witnesses employed by one party) invalidates attestation for arbitration purposes.

### 3.3 Operational Logging Marks

**Context**: Field operations requiring accountability, safety verification, and outcome documentation.

**Required Documentation**:
- Operation type and commander/OWNER
- Personnel roster and roles
- Site location and environmental conditions
- Exposure windows and attenuation compliance
- Actions taken and critical decision points
- Black-flag triggers and abort conditions met/unmet
- Outcome and casualty disposition
- Equipment status and resource consumption
- Witness/recorder identity

**Applications**:
- Ruin-Stalker survey expeditions
- Silent Room Protocol interventions
- CPS Standard Test Suite administration
- Black Veins prospecting operations
- Combat formation drills and tactical operations
- Field experiments and diagnostic procedures

## 4.0 Recording Standards

### 4.1 Format Requirements

**Medium**: Permanent ink on parchment, leather, or approved synthetic. No chalk, charcoal, or erasable media for final marks.

**Legibility**: Block print or trained script. No personal shorthand unless accompanied by plaintext translation.

**Language**: Alfrune Standard or approved regional dialect. Technical terms must match Alfrune Lexicon definitions.

**Timestamp Format**:
- Date: Day-Month-Year (post-Glitch calendar)
- Time: Hour:Minute with watch designation (Morning/Afternoon/Evening/Night watch)
- Duration: Recorded for interventions and custody events

**Witness Signature**: Full name, role/affiliation, and personal mark or sigil. Anonymous marks are not valid.

### 4.2 Essential Fields (All Mark Types)

1. **Date and Timestamp**
2. **Location/Venue**
3. **Practitioner/Handler Identity**
4. **Subject/Exhibit Description**
5. **Action/Intervention Type**
6. **Outcome**
7. **Witness Identity and Signature**
8. **Adverse Events or Black-Flags** (if any)

### 4.3 Optional but Recommended Fields

- Environmental conditions (weather, lighting, ambient hazards)
- Equipment used and condition
- Costs expended (materials, stress, injury)
- Follow-up requirements or referrals
- Lessons learned or protocol deviations

## 5.0 Witness Selection and Qualification

### 5.1 Consent Substitution Witnesses

**Minimum Qualifications**:
- Field-certified member of recognized guild or creed
- Not under cognitive distress (CPS, panic, intoxication)
- Able to observe and record coherently
- No direct stake in intervention outcome

**Preferred**: Bone-Setters, Skalds, Kaupmaðr, or Rangers provide most reliable consent substitution witnessing due to training in neutral observation.

**Minimum Count**: One independent witness. Two or more preferred for high-risk interventions (Great Rites, experimental procedures, terminal prognosis cases).

### 5.2 Neutral Attestation Witness Pairs

**Minimum Qualifications**:
- Creed-neutral or representing opposing party interests
- Rangers Guild certification OR Grey Blades roster OR Dvergr guild standing
- No employment relationship with single party
- Clear of conflicts of interest

**Certification**: Rangers Guild maintains witness certification program with annual renewal. Grey Blades and Dvergr guilds recognize equivalent internal certifications.

**Minimum Count**: Two witnesses ("witness pair"). Solo attestation is not valid for arbitration or custody purposes.

### 5.3 Operational Logging Witnesses

**Minimum Qualifications**:
- Member of operation team OR independent observer
- Literate and trained in recording protocols
- Not incapacitated during recording window

**Role Assignment**: Operation commander designates recorder at mission start. Recorder responsibility rotates if primary becomes incapacitated.

**Minimum Count**: One designated recorder. Critical operations (ruin entry, hazard containment, combat) should maintain redundant recorders when feasible.

## 6.0 Timing and Recording Procedures

### 6.1 Real-Time Recording (Preferred)

**When Feasible**: Record witness marks during or immediately following the event while details remain fresh.

**Procedure**:
1. Practitioner/handler announces intent and begins action
2. Witness observes and begins contemporaneous notes
3. Upon completion, practitioner and witness jointly complete mark template
4. Both sign and timestamp
5. Mark is filed per custody requirements

### 6.2 Retrospective Recording (Crisis Standard)

**When Required**: Real-time recording impossible due to immediate danger, cognitive load, or lack of materials.

**Procedure**:
1. Complete intervention/action using all available cognitive resources
2. As soon as safe and feasible (≤4 hours strongly preferred, ≤24 hours maximum), convene practitioner and witness
3. Jointly reconstruct timeline and key decisions
4. Note "retrospective recording" and reason for delay
5. Both sign and timestamp recording session (not original event)
6. Mark is filed with delay explanation

**Quality Impact**: Retrospective marks carry reduced evidentiary weight in arbitration. Grey Blades may request corroborating testimony or physical evidence.

### 6.3 Batch Recording (Field Expeditions)

**When Appropriate**: Extended field operations with repeated similar actions (survey transects, serial diagnostics, convoy waypoint checks).

**Procedure**:
1. Establish template for repeated action type
2. Record essential variables for each instance (timestamp, location, outcome)
3. Witness reviews batch at watch end or daily (whichever is shorter)
4. Witness signs batch with notation of review scope
5. Anomalies or black-flags must be individually documented

**Limitation**: Not valid for consent substitution marks. Each mystic intervention requires individual witness mark.

## 7.0 Custody and Storage

### 7.1 Primary Custody

**Field Teams**: Team leader maintains custody of witness marks until return to scriptorium or guild hall.

**Clinical Settings**: Bone-Setter clinic or scriptorium archive receives marks within 48 hours.

**Custody Events**: Rangers Guild, Grey Blades, or Dvergr custodians receive marks immediately upon event completion.

### 7.2 Archive Requirements

**Filing**: Witness marks are filed by operation type, date, and practitioner/handler.

**Retention**: Minimum 7 years for routine operational marks. Minimum 20 years for consent substitution marks involving mystic interventions. Permanent retention for marks related to fatalities, arbitration, or experimental procedures.

**Access Control**: Witness marks containing personal medical information are restricted to practitioner, subject, authorized clinical reviewers, and legal proceedings. Neutral attestation marks are generally public unless sealed by arbitration order.

### 7.3 Dual-Entry Ledger System

For high-sensitivity operations (ruin entry, hazard containment, experimental research):

1. **Field Copy**: Practitioner/handler retains field notes and working witness marks
2. **Archive Copy**: Clean transcription filed with scriptorium or guild archive
3. **Reconciliation**: Ethics Officer or Archive Keeper periodically audits field and archive copies for consistency

**Anti-Corruption**: Dual-entry system prevents tampering, loss, or retrospective revision. Discrepancies trigger formal review.

## 9.0 Common Errors and Prevention

### 9.1 Incomplete Field Documentation

**Error**: Failing to record essential fields (timestamp, witness identity, outcome).

**Impact**: Witness mark may be rejected for arbitration or custody purposes. Ethical review may be triggered.

**Prevention**: Use standardized templates. Conduct post-operation reviews within 4 hours while memory is fresh.

### 9.2 Insufficient Witness Qualification

**Error**: Using witnesses with conflicts of interest, insufficient training, or who were cognitively compromised during event.

**Impact**: Neutral attestation is invalid. Consent substitution mark may not satisfy ethical review.

**Prevention**: Pre-identify qualified witnesses during operation planning. Maintain backup witness roster.

### 9.3 Excessive Delay in Recording

**Error**: Waiting >24 hours to complete retrospective witness marks.

**Impact**: Memory degradation reduces accuracy. Evidentiary weight is severely diminished.

**Prevention**: Treat witness mark completion as first priority after immediate danger has passed. Build recording windows into operational timelines.

### 9.4 Single-Witness Attestation for Custody

**Error**: Using single witness for custody transfer when witness pair is required.

**Impact**: Attestation is invalid for arbitration. Custody chain is legally broken.

**Prevention**: Plan custody operations to ensure two qualified witnesses are present. Delay non-emergency transfers if second witness unavailable.

### 9.5 Witness Observing vs. Participating

**Error**: Practitioner attempts to self-witness, or witness actively assists with intervention.

**Impact**: Witness independence is compromised. Mark may be rejected.

**Prevention**: Clearly delineate observer vs. participant roles at operation start. Observers record, do not act.
