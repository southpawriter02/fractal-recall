# ⚔️ Crusade Mobilization Procedures — Pure Forge Crossroads Year 781 PG

## Evidence Excerpt

**CRUSADE MOBILIZATION OBSERVATION**
Pure Forge Crossroads, Year 781 PG — Helheim Nest Crusade

### Observer Context

Jötun-Reader Keld Gray-Quill was embedded with Pure Forge Crossroads chapter during the mobilization of a Helheim Automata Nest Crusade. This observation documented the complete procedural chain from initial investigation through deployment—roughly three months of preparation transforming civilians into ideologically-committed Crusaders ready to face undead machines.

### Seven-Phase Mobilization Protocol

The Iron-Banes operate through a methodical seven-phase protocol that reveals much about their organizational discipline and theological integration:

**Phase 1 — Investigation:** The process begins with scout reports of potential Automata activity. An investigative team dispatches to confirm the threat and perform theological classification—determining whether the site warrants Crusade response or can be handled through lesser measures. This initial phase consumes roughly two weeks.

**Phase 2 — Theological Review:** The chapter's Convocation deliberates on the findings, drafting a formal Crusade Charter that establishes the theological justification, operational parameters, and resource commitments. The Charter requires unanimous approval from the senior Hammer-Brothers.

**Phase 3 — Call to Faith:** Public announcement transforms the Crusade from internal deliberation to community mobilization. Volunteers register their intent, undergo vetting for physical capability and theological commitment, and receive selection notification. This recruitment window spans approximately two weeks.

**Phase 4 — Logistical Preparation:** Overlapping with later recruitment, the chapter requisitions supplies, prepares equipment, and coordinates with allied factions. Rangers Guild liaisons are secured for navigation and scouting. Combine militias volunteer auxiliary support. This logistical buildup requires nearly a month.

**Phase 5 — Ritual Consecration:** The spiritual preparation phase involves purification rites for all participants, blessing of weapons at the chapter forge, and formal oath-taking ceremonies. Each Crusader commits their life to the Hammer's purpose.

**Phase 6 — March Discipline:** The march to target spans roughly ten days, with daily liturgy reinforcing theological commitment. Strict protocols govern rest, meals, and interaction with populations encountered along the route.

**Phase 7 — Engagement Rules:** Final pre-briefing establishes tactical assignments. Combat proceeds until target destruction is complete. The Final Rite—ritual purification of the site—concludes the Crusade.

### Force Composition

The Crusade assembled a substantial force representing the full breadth of Iron-Bane organizational structure:

The core consisted of Hammer-Brothers serving leadership, combat, and ritual roles, supported by Hammer-Acolytes handling logistics, support functions, and medical aid. The bulk of the force comprised Congregational Crusaders—vetted volunteers from the chapter's lay congregation who had completed the Call to Faith selection process.

Rangers Guild liaisons provided navigation, scouting, and tactical advice—a pragmatic accommodation that Iron-Bane doctrine permits when facing targets outside familiar territory. Combine militia volunteers rounded out the force with allied auxiliary support.

### Resource Allocation

The chapter committed substantial resources to the endeavor. The treasury provided the majority of funding, supplemented by congregational donations from the faithful who could not march but wished to contribute to the Hammer's work.

Combat equipment included sledgehammers, pry-bars, and hand-axes—the traditional Iron-Bane arsenal suited for destroying Automata frames. Climbing harnesses, protective aprons, and portable field forges supported sustained operations. Extensive provisions, water supplies, tents, and bedrolls sustained the force through the march and engagement period.

Ritual materials held particular significance: consecrated Pure Steel spikes for Final Rite completion, forge-ash for purification ceremonies, and Hammer-and-Anvil standards identifying the chapter's presence. Transportation relied on supply carts and draft animals, with riding horses reserved for command elements and Rangers liaisons.

### Engagement Outcome

The Crusade achieved its objectives. The Helheim Automata Nest was destroyed completely—all structures demolished, the overwhelming majority of Automata neutralized with only a handful escaping into the surrounding terrain.

The human cost reflected what Iron-Bane doctrine terms "the Hammer's price"—casualties accepted as theologically non-negotiable when pursuing righteous destruction of the Machine-Dead. Injuries affected roughly one in five participants, with fatalities claiming a smaller but significant portion of the force. The Final Rite was completed on schedule, consecrating the cleansed site against future Automata occupation.

### Theological Significance

This mobilization demonstrates the Iron-Bane capacity to transform civilian populations into committed fighting forces through structured theological and logistical preparation. The three-month timeline—from initial scout report to Final Rite completion—represents their standard operational tempo for mid-scale Crusade operations.
