## 1.0 Gutter-Skald Network Structure (Observed)

### 1.1 Visible Hierarchy

**Stall Operators ("Tongue-Wagglers"):** Public-facing information vendors. Maintain fixed positions in Information Sector. Handle low-value transactions.

**Runners ("Whisper-Feet"):** Mobile agents who circulate through Great Market, collecting fresh intelligence from arriving caravans. Identified by distinctive ear-cuffs (copper wire, left ear).

**Brokers ("Coin-Tongues"):** Senior operators who handle high-value transactions. Not visible in public stalls; accessed through referral only.

### 1.2 Information Classification System

Gutter-Skalds categorize merchandise using a three-tier system:

| Category | Description | Typical Price Range |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Road-Whispers** | Route conditions, hazard reports, convoy schedules | 0.5-3 Cog-Units |
| **Hearth-Whispers** | Personal intelligence, political rumors, factional movements | 5-25 Cog-Units |
| **Gate-Whispers** | High-value secrets (assassination plots, creedal operations, Hush anomalies) | 50+ Cog-Units (negotiated) |

## 2.0 Transaction Observation

### 2.1 Subject Profile

**Buyer:** Dvergr merchant (Iron-Cartel insignia visible); seeking route intelligence for Glimmerheim-bound cargo
**Seller:** "Tongue-Waggler" operating from Stall NE-12-C; female, approximately 40 years; missing two fingers on left hand (old injury)

### 2.2 Transaction Sequence

**Phase 1 — Approach Protocol**

Buyer approaches stall. Seller initiates with standard greeting: *"What wind brings you, traveler?"* Buyer responds with coded phrase: *"A cold wind from below."* (Indicates Dvergr origin; Svartalfheim route interest.)

**Phase 2 — Commodity Negotiation**

Buyer requests: *"Road-Whispers for the Glimmerheim corridor. Fresh—no older than ten days."*

Seller offers three tiers:
1. **Basic Package (1.5 CU):** General route status, known hazard zones, estimated travel time
2. **Caravan Package (3 CU):** Basic + recent convoy reports, patrol schedules, resupply point conditions
3. **Premium Package (6 CU):** Caravan + rumored threats, political developments at destination, competitor movements

Buyer selects Caravan Package after brief haggling (final price: 2.5 CU).

**Phase 3 — Delivery Protocol**

Seller does not provide information directly. Instead, issues a small clay token with scratched symbol. Instructs buyer: *"Present this at the Broken Anvil tavern. Ask for 'the one who remembers.' Speak your need. Burn the token after."*

**Phase 4 — Verification**

Buyer asks: *"How do I know the whispers are true?"*

Seller responds with apparent standard reply: *"The Skalds remember everything. We remember who paid for lies. Bad whispers cost more than coin—they cost tongues."*

## 3.0 Diagnostic Analysis

### 3.1 Operational Security

The token-based delivery system serves multiple purposes:
- **Deniability:** Stall operators never directly provide intelligence
- **Verification:** Tokens can be tracked; repeat customers receive better service
- **Enforcement:** Forged tokens identified and traced back to counterfeiters

### 3.2 Economic Model

Gutter-Skalds operate on a **commission-and-contribution** model:
- Runners contribute fresh intelligence to shared pool
- Stall operators pay percentage of sales to network
- Brokers handle high-value exclusive transactions; pay protection to network leadership

### 3.3 Relationship to Ratatoskr Syndicate

Unconfirmed connection observed: Runner with copper ear-cuff seen entering building marked with Ratatoskr sigil (squirrel clutching acorn). Possible organizational overlap or tributary relationship.

---

## 4.0 Assessment

The Gutter-Skald network represents a sophisticated, decentralized intelligence apparatus operating in plain sight within Crossroads' legitimate commerce. Their information is generally reliable—the network's long-term viability depends on reputation.

**Recommendation:** Maintain observation; do not attempt infiltration. Network's enforcement mechanisms ("bad whispers cost tongues") appear to be more than rhetorical.
