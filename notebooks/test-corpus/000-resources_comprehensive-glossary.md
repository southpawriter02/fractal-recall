# Comprehensive Aethelgardian Glossary

## A - Core Terms

**Aesir** - The "Gods" of Order and Technology. Historically, the Technocratic ruling class of the Age of Forging. In Layer 1 (Common), spoken of as distant, sleeping, or dead gods. In Layer 3, refers to the corporate/scientific administration.

**Aether** - The foundational energy-matter field of Aethelgard. Corrupted by the **Ginnungagap Glitch** into a carrier wave for paradoxical anti-logic (**Runic Blight**). Known as "The Breath of the World" and "The operating system of reality."

**Alchemy** - The craft of combining plants, minerals, and salvaged chemicals to create potions, salves, and poisons. Distinct from magic; relies on memorized formulas and "coherent chemistry." Not magical; governed by WITS.

**Álfar** - "Elves". Post-human divergences mutated by Aether exposure. Divided into **Ljósálfar** (Light) and **Dökkálfar** (Dark). Not magical beings, but mutated survivors.

**Alka-hestur** - "Combat Alchemist". A Skirmisher specialization focusing on "coherent chemistry" and payload delivery via lance. Uses science, not magic.

**Anvil-Cant** - The weaponized, binary language of the Iron-Bane Crusade. Designed to eradicate nuance and promote aggression. Hard, declarative, aggressive. Replaces complex concepts with "Pure" vs "Corrupted".

**Ash-Vargr** - Large, feral wolves adapted to ruin environments with ash-colored camouflage. "The patient, hungry earth." Dangerous pack hunters.

**Astral Projection** - "The Severed Soul". Dangerous technique using **Silver Cord** interfaces to project consciousness into the Blighted Aether. Originally for remote piloting (Einherjar). Now a high-risk dive into psychic static.

## B - Beings and Beasts

**Berserkr** - "The Roaring Fire". A Warrior specialization that channels psychic trauma/static into physical rage (Fury). Heretical path with high risk/reward. "A conduit of the Scream."

**Beast-Binder** - A Skirmisher specialization that forms a symbiotic, corrupted bond with a Blighted beast (Ash-Vargr, Rune-Bear, etc.). "The Symbiotic Interface." Heretical partnership.

**Blight-Weaver** - See Seidĸkona. A mystic who has embraced Runic Blight to achieve reality-warping capabilities.

**Bifrost** - The wormhole transit network connecting the Nine Decks. Subject to catastrophic failure during the Glitch. Now known as "The Heimdallr Signal," a permanent broadcast of raw, paradoxical instruction.

## C - Cants and Communication

**Cog-Grind** - The relentless cycle of salvage labor and servitude. Cynical, exhausted reference to both physical work and crushing systemic oppression.

**Cog-Worshipper** - Derogatory term for **God-Sleepers** from Anvil-Cant. Implies blasphemous worship of machines.

**CPS** - **Cognitive Paradox Syndrome**. Also "Static Sickness". Informational disease caused by exposure to the **Runic Blight**. "The Sickness of the Soul." "Ruin-Madness."

## D - Depths and Dökkálfar

**Dökkálfar** - "Dark Elves". Aether-mutated post-humans of **Svartalfheim**. Pale, light-sensitive, attuned to "Dead Zones" and psychic echoes. "The Echo-Born." Reclusive mystics.

**Draugr-Pattern** - "Huscarl-Class". Security/Combat automata designed for pacification. Powered by Iron Hearts. "The walking ghosts of a dead law." Aggressive, patrol routes.

**Dvergr** - The Dwarves. Subterranean masters of engineering and mining. Geomechanical lifeforms animated by the earth's resonance. Never "Dwarfs".

**Dökkálfar** - "Dark Elves". Post-human divergences in **Svartalfheim**. Pale, light-sensitive, attuned to "Dead Zones" and psychic echoes.

## E - Energy and Echoes

**Echo-Cant** - The acoustic spatial-mapping "language" of the Silent Folk. Consists of clicks and echolocation. Represented as complex click-streams translating to spatial data.

**Echo-Thirst** - Obsessive focus on salvage and "King's Finds"; a form of adaptive pathology/madness. Describes someone losing themselves to greed/desperation of salvage.

**Einbúi** - "Hermit" or "Loner". A Ranger specialization focusing on radical self-reliance and generalist survival skills. "The Ultimate Survivor." "Ghost in the Broken World."

## F - Factions and Forces

**Forsaken** - "Ice-Thralls". The indentured population of Niflheim, bound by **Ice-Debt** to Scavenger Barons. "The Rusted Chain." Desperate, fatalistic survivors.

**Fenrir-Pattern** - Designation for pre-Glitch bio-engineered apex predators. Ancestors of the Great Wolf.

**Fimbul-Cant** - The pidgin dialect of the Niflheim Forsaken (Ice-Debt thralls). Focused on cold, debt, and labor. Precise about cold/suffering. Fatalistic.

## G - Glitch and God

**Ginnungagap (Glitch)** - The "Great Glitch" or "Reality Tear". The catastrophic event that ended the Age of Forging. Layer 1: A maw/abyss. Layer 2: A fatal exception/system crash.

**God-Sleeper** - A religious faction revering the **Jötun-Forged** and **Undying** as sleeping deities. Belief in the "Great Awakening." "The Pilgrim's Tongue." Ritualistic, faithful, pacifist (mostly).

**Gutter-Cant** - The trade pidgin of the Rust-Clans and Scavengers. Efficient, transactional, and slang-heavy. Used in Utgard and the Wastes.

## I - Iron and Identity

**Iron-Bane** - A faction of militant purists ("The Crusade") dedicated to destroying all "corrupted" technology and Aetheric influence. "Anvil-Pounders." Zealous, uncompromising.

**Iron-Husk** - Derogatory term for the **Undying** (Utility Servitors). Dehumanizing. "Purge the Iron-Husks."

**Iron Heart** - A perpetual Aetheric power core (Ingwaz rune). Valuable salvage. Iron-Banes call it a "Demon Heart".

**Iron-Husk Rider** - A Skirmisher specialization that bonds with and rides a corrupted mechanical beast (Svin-fylking). "Living Avalanche." Heretical synthesis.

## J - Jötun and Journey

**Jarl** - The ruler of a Midgard **Hold**. Authority derived from strength and ability to protect the community. "The Jarl's Axe is the final law."

**Jötun** - Giants. Mythological name for the J.T.N. Series Servitors (terraforming machines). Pronounced "YOH-tun". Used in sagas and common speech.

**Jötun-Reader** - A scholar/analyst specialization dedicated to deciphering the past (The Great Work). "System Analyst" / "Grey-Robe". Intellectual, observant, diagnostic.

**Jötunheim** - The Realm of Giants. An industrial wasteland of colossal ruins. Home of the Rust-Clans and most dangerous salvage.

**Jötun-Forged** - The massive industrial constructs and infrastructure left by the J.T.N. Series. Revered by God-Sleepers. "The Sleeping Gods." Dormant megastructures.

## L - Layers and Light

**Ljósálfar** - "Light Elves". Aether-mutated post-humans of the surface. Tall, luminescent features, attuned to "living" Aether. "The Star-Touched."

**Lys-Alfar** - "Rune-Sprites". Sentient fragments of corrupted code/data that manifest as swarms of light. "Data-Wraiths." Feed on hamingja.

**Lys-Seker** - "Glimmer-Drinkers". Mystics who embrace Glimmer-Madness to commune with the shattered Aether. "Prophets of the Paradox."

## M - Machines and Mystics

**Midgard** - "The Middle Realm". The central inhabited region. Home to Holds, Hearth-Clans, and most human settlements. "The Heart of the World."

**Muspelheim** - The Fire Realm. A volcanic wasteland of lava flows and geothermal vents. Home of the Hearth-Clans.

**Myrk-gengr** - "Shadow-Walker" or "Dark-Walker". A stealth/assassin specialization. "Ghost in the Static."

**Myr-Stalker** - "Entropic Predator". A Ranger specialization that weaponizes the **Runic Blight's** physical corruption. "Antibody of a Dying World."

## N - Niflheim and Norms

**Niflheim** - "The Frozen Tomb". A realm of eternal ice, Cryo-Preservation ruins, and Scavenger Barons. Home of the Forsaken and Dreadnoughts.

**Nidavellir** - The Forges of the Dvergr. A vast subterranean network of mines, workshops, and resonance chambers. "The Deep Anvil." Dvergr homeland.

**Norn-Spinner** - A rare, potent Mystic specialization. "Weavers of the Chaotic Wyrd." Actively rewrite probability/causality (Source Code). "The Scripter of a Broken Reality." High Corruption risk.

## O - O.D.I.N. and Order

**O.D.I.N.** - The "All-Father". Originally the Omniscient Directive Initiative Network (AI Matrix). Layer 1: The King of Gods. Layer 3: The governing AI.

**Örlög-Bound** - "Fate-Touched". Individuals with unstable Bio-Aetheric Fields who passively warp probability ("Luck-Tide"). "Walking glitches."

## R - Realms and Runes

**Raidō** - Rune of the Journey, Wagon, or Wheel. Function: Vectored Motion / Trajectory. Associated with travel, transport, and paths.

**Realm** - One of the nine major world-divisions of Aethelgard (e.g., Midgard, Jötunheim). Each contains multiple **Biomes**.

**Rune** - A symbol of power encoding Aetheric effects. Can be carved, inscribed, or sung (Galdr). "The Language of the World." Functional programming for reality.

**Runic Blight** - "The Sickness of Reality". Permanent, ambient, paradoxical "noise" (anti-logic) left by the Ginnungagap Glitch. "The Serpent's Final Venom."

**Rune-Bear** - A bear mutated by Aetheric absorption, displaying glowing runic patterns in its fur. Magical beast.

**Rust-Clans** - "Nomads of the Iron Sea". Inhabitants of **Jötunheim**. Culturally defined by the "Creed of the Self". Pragmatic, cynical, self-reliant.

## S - Seidr and Silence

**Seiðkona / Seiðmaðr** - "Seer-Woman" / "Seer-Man". A Mystic specialization practicing **Galdr** (sung magic) and ritual runecraft. "The Singer." High-risk, high-reward spellcaster.

**Silent Folk** - The Dökkálfar subculture of Svartalfheim who have abandoned spoken language entirely in favor of **Echo-Cant**. "The Unspoken." Alien and inscrutable.

**Silver Cord** - A bio-neural interface technology used for remote piloting (**Astral Projection**). Prone to catastrophic feedback. "The Tether."

**Skald** - "The Voice of Memory". A support specialization focusing on morale, inspiration, and oral history preservation. "The Fire-Speaker."

**Svartalfheim** - The Shadow Realm. A lightless underground network of caves and ruins inhabited by Dökkálfar and Silent Folk. "The Clicking Deeps."

## T - Trail and Thrall

**Trail-Cant** - The symbolic marking system used by Rangers to communicate warnings, directions, and resources. Visual language. Carved or painted symbols.

**Undying** - Collective term for functional automata and servitors. Revered by **God-Sleepers**. "The Sleepers." Active machines from the Age of Forging.

**Utgard** - "The Outer Yards". Lawless scavenger settlements on the edge of Jötunheim. Dangerous frontier towns. "The Edge." Where the desperate go.

## V - Vanaheim and Valleys

**Vanaheim** - "The Overgrown Garden". A realm of rampaging, mutated biological growth. Home of the Grove-Clans and Huldrefolk.

**Vargr-Claw** - A Trail-Cant symbol (Ψ) warning of Ash-Vargr presence. Often modified with dots or circles to indicate pack size or activity time.

**Yggdrasil** - The World Tree. Originally the planetary network/infrastructure. Pronounced "IG-drah-sil". The structure connecting the Realms.
