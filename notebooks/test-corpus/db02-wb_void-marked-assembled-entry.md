## Diagnostic Summary

The **Void-Marked** are individuals who have survived sustained Aetheric Blight exposure and emerged with permanent physical markers, altered perception, and measurable Aetheric resonance signatures. Unlike the Harrow-Sick — who deteriorate under similar exposure — the Void-Marked achieve a stable symbiosis with corrupted Aetheric energy. This divergent outcome remains one of the most significant unsolved mysteries in post-Glitch medical science.

The Void-Marked are not a faction in the conventional sense. They share no unified ideology, governance structure, or territorial claims. They are bound only by physiological transformation and the social consequences that follow. Their status — whether citizens, refugees, threats, or sacred beings — varies dramatically across factional boundaries.

---

## §1 — Physical Characteristics

The defining markers from which the Void-Marked take their name are visible alterations to skin, eyes, and body structure that develop during or shortly after the Aetheric exposure event:

**Dermal Void-Lines:** Dark, fractal-branching patterns visible beneath the skin surface, following neither vascular nor neural pathways. Unlike the iron deposits of Harrow-Sick, Void-Lines are composed of stabilised Aetheric resonance patterns integrated into subcutaneous tissue. They emit a faint, barely perceptible luminescence in complete darkness.

**Ocular Transformation:** Iris pigmentation shifts to deep violet or silver-white, with pupil dilation responding to Aetheric field intensity rather than light levels. Advanced cases demonstrate low-level involuntary Aetheric perception — the ability to "see" active runic inscriptions, Spell-Lock resonance fields, and Blight-concentration gradients.

**Neurological Adaptation:** EEG-equivalent testing (conducted via Dvergr resonance measurement) reveals novel neural oscillation patterns not present in baseline human subjects. The Void-Marked brain appears to have developed dedicated processing architecture for Aetheric data — a biological parallel to the Æther-Weave OS sensor integration.

**Metabolic Stability:** Despite carrying measurable Aetheric load, Void-Marked individuals show no progressive deterioration. Cellular repair mechanisms appear to have incorporated Aetheric energy as a metabolic substrate rather than rejecting it as toxic contamination. Lifespan data is insufficient for long-term prognosis — the oldest confirmed Void-Marked is 74 years post-exposure.

---

## §2 — Origins and Exposure Pathways

**No deliberate creation methodology exists.** Void-Marking occurs spontaneously during acute or chronic Blight exposure events. Known pathways include:

- **Salvage exposure:** Workers in Asgard-periphery ruins encountering concentrated Aetheric pockets
- **Blight-Storm survival:** Rare individuals who survive direct Blight-Storm passage without Harrow-Sick onset
- **Proximity to active ruins:** Long-term habitation near functional pre-Glitch Aetheric infrastructure
- **Natal exposure:** Children born to Void-Marked parents show ~15% incidence of congenital Void-Marking

**Conversion rate:** Of all individuals experiencing significant acute Blight exposure, approximately 1 in 50 develop Void-Marking rather than Harrow-Sick. The determining factors remain unknown. Genetic predisposition is suspected but unconfirmed.

---

## §3 — Social Status and Political Contestation

The Void-Marked occupy a uniquely contested position in post-Glitch society:

**Warden-Host Protection Doctrine:**

The Warden-Host — a paramilitary sanctuary organization — maintains the position that Void-Marked are protected persons deserving full citizenship rights and sanctuary access. Warden-Host sanctuaries serve as primary safe havens for Void-Marked populations. This protectionist stance creates significant tension with factions that view the Void-Marked as threats.

**Iron-Bane Crusade — Contamination Classification:**

Iron-Bane doctrine classifies Void-Marked as "Blight-Vessels" — living contamination sources that pose existential risk to human purity. Crusade territory enforces exclusion policies; Void-Marked individuals found within Iron-Bane Holds face forced exile and, in extreme cases, "purification" — a euphemism for execution. The theological justification: _"The Blight cannot be worn. It can only corrupt. Those who carry its mark carry its hunger."_

**Hearth-Clan Ambivalence:**

Midgard Hold responses vary dramatically. Progressive Holds integrate Void-Marked populations, valuing their Aetheric perception abilities for threat detection and salvage assessment. Conservative Holds enforce quarantine districts or outright exclusion, citing fears of ambient Aetheric radiation.

**Jötun-Reader Scientific Interest:**

The Medical Division regards the Void-Marked as the most important living research population in post-Glitch pathology. Understanding the mechanism that produces stable Aetheric integration — rather than Harrow-Sick degeneration — could potentially yield treatments or preventive measures for Blight exposure.

---

## §4 — Capabilities and Operational Utility

**Aetheric Perception:** Void-Marked individuals can detect Aetheric field gradients, active runic inscriptions, and Blight concentration levels without instrumentation. This ability makes them invaluable as salvage scouts, hazard mappers, and Spell-Lock evaluators.

**Blight Tolerance:** Complete resistance to further Aetheric degradation enables operations in environments lethal to baseline humans. Void-Marked scouts can safely enter zones that would produce Stage 2 Harrow-Sick in unprotected personnel within hours.

**Resonance Communication:** Some advanced Void-Marked report the ability to sense other Void-Marked at distances up to several hundred metres — a poorly understood phenomenon that has led to informal "network" coordination among scattered populations.

---

## §5 — Material Interactions

The Void-Marked body interacts anomalously with certain materials:

**Weir-Bone:** Contact with Weir-Bone artifacts produces measurable intensification of Void-Line luminescence and temporary enhancement of Aetheric perception range. The interaction is non-destructive to either party and has led to Weir-Bone being used in Void-Marked-specific diagnostic and protective equipment.

**Iron Husk:** Proximity to Iron Husk material creates conflicting resonance patterns — mild discomfort to moderate pain depending on concentration. This aversion is involuntary and purely physiological; it provides no diagnostic or operational utility but does create complications in salvage contexts.

**Standard metals:** No anomalous interactions. Void-Marked handle conventional tools, weapons, and equipment without issue.
