## 1.0 Purpose, Scope, Audience

### 1.1 Purpose

This manual defines the lawful method for establishing, operating, and terminating psycho‑aetheric synthesis between an authorized operator and a designated J.T.N. Frame via the Argentum‑Nexus ("Silver Cord"). Its immediate objective is identity preservation under load. Its broader objective is deterministic throughput: to convert human intent, bounded by protocol, into machine actuation without ambiguity or loss of self.

The system's success criterion is strictly binary: either a complete, verified identity bridge with bounded latency and intact cognitive function is achieved, or the attempt is aborted by design. Ambiguity is classified as hazard and is treated as system failure pending remediation.

### 1.2 Scope

This document governs:

- Pre‑interface preparation and validation of operator state and throne assembly
- Connection sequence, stabilization, and initial sensory gating
- Operational envelopes for combat and industrial synthesis under O.D.I.N. arbitration
- Standard hazard protocols, maintenance, and disengagement procedures
- Compliance, consent, archival coupling, and audit discipline

This document explicitly excludes Vanir biosymbiosis stacks and Dvergr guild interfaces. Cross‑faction hardware or doctrine is non‑compatible by design and outside the authority of this manual.

### 1.3 Audience and Roles

- **Einherjar Operator:** the human cognitive nucleus during synthesis. During a valid session, the operator functions as the primary high‑order processor for Frame systems while remaining subordinate to safety governors and policy emissions from O.D.I.N.
- **Cadre Technician:** the responsible authority for throne inspection, protocol table loads, pre‑ and post‑session checks, incident capture, and quarantine actions. The Cadre enforces abort ladders without exception.
- **Oversight AI Endpoints:** O.D.I.N. for global arbitration and "System Harmony" attestation. Security handshakes with HEIMDALL enforce paradox‑lock constraints on transit and access. Automated systems are not "advisors." They are governors with defined writs.

### 1.4 Definitions (Operational)

- **Hamingja:** the operator's Bio‑Aetheric Field, treated as a measurable identity signature and the basis for attestation.
- **Argentum‑Nexus (Silver Cord):** the identity‑coherence mesh and psycho‑aetheric bus enabling total synthesis and archival imaging.
- **O.D.I.N.:** Omniscient Directive Initiative Network, the distributed governance lattice that issues policy to subordinate systems, including J.T.N. Frames.
- **CPS (Cognitive Paradox Syndrome):** clinical degradation under paradoxical or anti‑logical stimulus. In this manual, CPS appears only as an exposure class with thresholds and screens; speculation is prohibited.

### 1.5 Safety Doctrine

Improvisation is a hazard class. Procedures are instruments, not suggestions. Where human preference conflicts with protocol timing, protocol executes. The design ethic is as follows:

- Perfection ⇒ stability ⇒ morality. Safety governors pre‑empt human intent when limits are met.
- Abort ladders are virtue, not failure. A clean abort preserves identity and system integrity.
- Data primacy: logs, checksums, and attestation are the sole arbiters of truth; recollection is not admissible as evidence.

### 1.6 Success Criteria and Abort Conditions

- **Success** is declared only upon receipt of O.D.I.N. "System Harmony Achieved," sub‑threshold command latency, verified hamingja lock, and nominal sensory gating. Any missing condition mandates rollback.
- **Abort** is mandated on detection of Frayed‑Cord indicators, unmitigated cognitive feedback (Draugr's Echo), unscreened Data‑Wraith signatures, or governor‑flagged instability in the Runic Power Core. All aborts transition immediately to capture, quarantine, and screening.

## 3.0 Pre‑Interface Protocols

### 3.1 Operator State Validation

All synthesis attempts begin with the operator, not the machine. The Cadre conducts a full‑spectrum biometric and hamingja resonance scan in the calibration chamber. Variance against the operator's baseline is evaluated across physiological, neurological, and psycho‑aetheric markers. Deviations beyond approved tolerances constitute a system variable, not a personal failing, and mandate deferral. Abstinence and sleep mandates are verified by log; self‑attestation is non‑admissible.

Acceptance criteria are binary: within window or out of window. Sub‑threshold variances are not "close enough"; they are precursors to cognitive feedback and partial identity states. Operators cleared under this gate are authorized to proceed to conditioning without exception handling.

### 3.2 Conditioning and Directive Recitation

The Neural Resonator entrains the operator's signal to a narrowband focus suitable for protocol execution. During entrainment, the operator performs a complete, verbatim recitation of core directives and hazard ladders. The goal is not persuasion but alignment: procedures must execute faster than preference. Hesitation is classified as a timing error. Any stumble, pause, or interpretive paraphrase resets the sequence and, on repetition, results in grounding.

Upon successful completion, the Resonator issues a green readiness flag. That flag is a precondition to authorizing throne access. No alternate path exists.

### 3.3 Command Throne Inspection

The throne is treated as a sterile instrument. Ports are inspected for micrometric seal integrity. Ozone trace, crystalline residue, or thermal signature anomalies constitute disqualifying faults for the present cycle. Synapse array health is verified by parity tests; dead channels are remediated by cold‑swap before the operator is permitted to seat. Tool and solvent use is limited to approved chemistries; ad‑hoc cleaning introduces uncontrolled variables and is prohibited.

If any item fails inspection, the Cadre locks out the throne and logs the fault. The operator is not seated. Post‑facto heroics are not recognized categories in this program.

### 3.4 Environment and Warding Stack

Synthesis occurs within an environment designed to minimize noise and bleed. The chamber is cleared for EMI and psycho‑aetheric contamination. Memetic baffles are engaged. Personnel shielding is staged to damp residual field effects without occluding necessary telemetry. Squad‑mesh channels are placed in a quiescent state; only safety and actuation lanes will be enabled at handshake.

Pre‑interface concludes with a three‑point confirmation: Cadre instrument sign‑off, operator verbal readiness, and environment green across all gates. Only upon receipt of all three does the system advance to the connection sequence.

## 6.0 Hazard Protocols

### 6.1 Draugr's Echo (Cognitive Feedback)

Draugr's Echo is a self‑reinforcing loop between ungoverned affective noise and the psycho‑aetheric channel. It begins in the operator and is amplified by the system if not arrested. Early telemetry presents as spectral jitter across the Manifold band, micro‑tremor on fine actuators, and a low‑amplitude audio artifact in the operator channel. The correct response is procedural and immediate: engage Quieting Script Q‑ALG‑MIND‑01; throttle all discretionary telemetry while preserving safety and actuation lanes; induce low‑tone resonance via the Resonator. If spectral jitter persists beyond the allotted window, the session is soft‑disconnected. No heroic concentration, no "riding it out." This is not a contest of will; it is an instrument returning to safe tolerances.

Post‑incident, the operator submits to a Class‑Alpha CPS screen and a short grounding interval followed by directive recall. Clean metrics and perfect recitation are prerequisites for re‑attempt.

### 6.2 Frayed Cord (Partial Severance)

A Frayed Cord is an incomplete identity bridge. Its causes are mechanical or environmental—micrometric seal faults, synapse channel dropouts, electromagnetic spikes—not character. Telemetry reveals the failure: command latency drifts into the red; the visual field erupts in static; O.D.I.N. escalates handshake retries. The countermeasure is Yggdrasil Blackout. Keys are revoked, the bridge collapses, buffers are purged, ports are hard‑locked. This sequence executes within a quarter second and is not negotiable.

Following Blackout, the Cadre enforces lockout‑tagout on the throne and extracts the operator inside the defined window. Re‑link within twenty‑four hours is prohibited. Mechanical root cause is found and remediated; the fix is validated in a clean‑room synthetic session prior to live interface. Any alternative practice invites Husk‑Corruption and is a violation of doctrine.

### 6.3 Data‑Wraith Intrusion (External Data‑Ghost)

A Data‑Wraith is hostile alien data attempting to run inside the operator's channel. Its attack surface includes dormant trunk lines, contaminated archives, and Alfheim residues. Indicators are high‑entropy floods uncorrelated to mission traffic, paradox tokens in the log, and the operator's subjective compulsion to interpret.

Procedure is conservative and absolute. Erect an Algiz fence at maximum projection. Air‑gap the operator's cognitive channel by cutting the squad‑mesh. Under no condition does the operator read, listen to, or visualize the payload. If the flood persists or any compulsion is reported, initiate soft disconnect and escrow all telemetry. Decoding occurs only in air‑gapped, warded facilities. Curiosity is an exposure vector; prohibition is the control.

### 6.4 Runic Power Core Instability (Anvil's Whisper)

The Runic Power Core reports its unease before it fails. The first sign is an ultrasonic "whisper," rising in slope, paired with atmospheric scintillation and minor power bus spikes. The Frame is a contained star; changes in its voice matter. Drop non‑essential loads and assume low‑governor mode. Engage Eihwaz‑class smoothing and observe the bus. If spikes persist beyond tolerance, disengage to stasis and evacuate the perimeter. Treat resultant phenomena as structural hazards, not as puzzles. A localized reality‑quake is a mechanical event with metaphysical consequences; it is not a battlefield to be won.

### 6.5 Theoretical Paradox Event (Reference Only)

This subsection exists for completeness and is not operational. The All‑Rune class paradox represents a statistically negligible risk under existing safeguards. Exposure at a live interface yields instantaneous cognitive erasure. Containment is architectural: paradox‑locks, sandbox isolation, and governance quorums. Operators receive no field directives for this scenario beyond recognition that no action is possible. Detailed materials are held under rev‑locked DAGAZ containment memoranda.

### 6.6 Psychic Stress Accumulation (Weight of Knowing)

Even under pristine conditions, extended synthesis imposes cumulative cognitive load. The onset begins as a harmless "static hiss" and progresses to fatigue, narrowed attention, and intrusive ideation. The defense is scheduling. Combat sessions are bounded to ninety minutes, industrial sessions to one hundred twenty, followed by a mandatory decohere window under Resonator and Kvasir quieting. Readouts are taken every quarter hour; CPS screens bookend each cycle. Escalation to a Beta‑class profile triggers a day of grounding and supervised re‑conditioning.

The ethic governing all hazard response is consistent: procedures pre‑empt preference; measured states outrank impressions; a clean abort is success by another name.

## 7.0 Maintenance and Calibration

### 7.1 Ports and Seals

The throne's ports are precision interfaces. Clean using approved, non‑reactive solvent A‑17 with lint‑free swabs. Alcohols and improvised agents are prohibited. After cleaning, perform a phased‑ultrasound pass; any Δphase greater than 0.002 at any port constitutes a reject for this cycle. Replace seals at 500 synthesis cycles or on first detection of ozone trace or crystalline residue. Record all actions to the append‑only maintenance log with technician ID and timestamp.

### 7.2 Synapse Arrays

On boot, run channel parity tests across the synapse array. Dead or degraded channels are remediated by cold‑swap modules prior to seating. Maintain array temperature within ±0.3 K of the specified band; throttle and alarm if excursions persist beyond five seconds. Thermal variance is treated as a predictor of dropouts and is corrected before interface authorization.

### 7.3 Protocol Tables (Rune Kernel)

Protocol tables are version‑pinned per mission profile. Update only with signed quorum authorization. Rollbacks require the same quorum and produce a REV entry detailing rationale, impacted procedures, and verification steps. No field edits, no "hot patches." The table in force at handshake governs the entire session.

### 7.4 Archival Integrity (Valhalla Coupling)

During active synthesis, delta‑image blocks are checksummed per write. Mismatches route to quarantine automatically. No live rendering of captured blocks may occur in human‑occupied zones. Post‑mission verification is performed in an air‑gapped facility under Cadre supervision. Consent flags stored with the mission contract govern retrieval scope and handling. Where archival integrity is in question, the proper response is to suspend review pending a clean recapture, not to extrapolate intent from damaged data.

### 7.5 Documentation and Audit

All maintenance actions, inspections, failures, replacements, and table changes are recorded to an append‑only ledger. The ledger is the system of record. Randomized audits verify conformance. Non‑conforming actions result in suspension of synthesis privileges until remediation and review are complete.

## 8.0 Contingencies and Disengagement

### 8.1 Yggdrasil Blackout (Hard Sever)

Blackout is the definitive response to identity‑bridge instability and paradox flags. It is designed to complete in a quarter second and to leave no partial state behind.

- **Preconditions:** any qualifying Frayed‑Cord indicators or paradox‑class flags raised by O.D.I.N. during or after handshake.
- **Execution Order:** revoke mesh keys → collapse the psycho‑aetheric bridge → purge residual buffers → assert hard mechanical lock on all ports → verify operator somatic baseline.
- **Operator Role:** remain passive. Attempting to "help" introduces delay. Cadre owns the sever.
- **Posture:** treat Blackout as success—identity preserved, system integrity maintained. The next step is diagnosis, not re‑attempt.

### 8.2 Emergency Local Cut‑Outs

If the throne fails to respond to Blackout or if mechanical damage prevents a clean sever, local cut‑outs are authorized.

- **Hardware:** physical sever points are located at lumbar and thoracic port junctions with color‑coded guards. Tools are staged within arm's reach of the Cadre.
- **Procedure:** engage lockout‑tagout before any blade meets metal. Cut in the marked order to avoid residual energization and to minimize tissue insult. Immediately apply port caps and dampers to prevent auto‑relink attempts.
- **Risk:** cut‑outs are destructive by design. Their value is certainty. Do not hesitate once invoked.

### 8.3 Post‑Incident Diagnostics and Recovery Posture

After any abnormal termination, data outranks memory.

- **Capture:** export handshake logs, fault codes, and relevant sensor traces to an escrow bundle. Tag with incident code and timestamp. Do not reopen the session to "see what went wrong."
- **Screening:** conduct CPS evaluation within fifteen minutes for all involved personnel, not just the operator. Apply grounding periods per variance class.
- **Root Cause:** remediate the mechanical, environmental, or table cause in a controlled setting. Validation occurs in a synthetic, air‑gapped harness before live interface is permitted again.

### 8.4 Notifications and Governance

- **Reporting:** emit incident codes to O.D.I.N. and the training or operations ledger as applicable. The disposition ladder determines re‑interface eligibility and any additional controls.
- **Records:** append all actions to the immutable REV log. Absence of a record is non‑compliance. Narrative recollections are not accepted in lieu of telemetry and authorized entries.
- **Ethics:** where archival blocks captured during the event are damaged or inconsistent, suspend review. Escalate to Cadre command for adjudication under consent flags and style governance.

## 9.0 Compliance and Ethics

### 9.1 Authority and Clearances

Authority is role‑bound and time‑bounded. Access to interface, maintenance, and override functions is granted by role‑based tokens that expire or are revoked automatically on role change. Every override records actor, scope, reason, and timestamp to an immutable ledger. There are no informal exceptions and no "temporary" side channels.

### 9.2 Consent and Archival Rights

Archival coupling operates under explicit consent flags set before mission start. These flags define capture scope, retrieval boundaries, and post‑mission handling. Consent applies prospectively. Blocks captured under valid configuration are governed by those terms and reviewed only in air‑gapped facilities under Cadre supervision. Personal curiosity is not a lawful basis for access.

### 9.3 Prohibited Practices

- Field modification of protocol tables or kernel ("hot patching").
- Improvisational galdr outside defined procedures.
- Cross‑faction hardware or doctrine grafts on Argentum‑Nexus or throne assemblies.
- Attempts to stabilize partial identity states by will or unsanctioned instrumentation.

Violations are treated first as hazards to identity and system integrity, then as disciplinary matters. Removal from interface eligibility is the default pending review.

### 9.4 Audit and Enforcement

Conformance is proven by telemetry and ledgers, not reputation. Randomized audits inspect maintenance logs, protocol histories, session traces, and incident handling. Findings result in remediation plans or suspension of synthesis privileges until corrective steps are verified. Dose thresholds, rest cadence, and CPS screens are compliance items; omissions are faults, not efficiencies.

### 9.5 Ethical Posture

This program's ethic is identity preservation and disciplined throughput. Clean aborts are successes; unlogged deviations are failures. Where immediate advantage conflicts with protocol integrity, protocol prevails. Excellence is the product of discipline, not of improvisation.
