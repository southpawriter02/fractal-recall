### INCIDENT TIMELINE

**T+00:00 (Hour 14:00) — Seidĸkona Sighting & Interdiction Deployment**

- **Location:** 3km south of Settlement Greenspire, edge of contaminated buffer zone
- **Civilian report:** Foraging party (5 Grove-Clan members) encountered Mirael Thornbloom in clearing
- **Description:** "Her eyes glowed violet. Her hair moved like it was underwater. The air around her shimmered. She was laughing, but the sound came from everywhere at once."
- **Foraging party fled immediately** (protocol: do not engage Seidĸkona)
- **Guardian Captain Thalia Greenward** notified Druidic Council (5-minute response time)
- **Warden Kaelen Darkroot** deployed Interdiction Team Alpha (8-minute mobilization)

**T+00:13 (Hour 14:13) — Interdiction Team Arrival & Initial Assessment**

- **Team approach:** Silent, from downwind, using canopy cover (standard Seidĸkona engagement tactics)
- **Visual confirmation:** Mirael Thornbloom standing in clearing, surrounded by 30m radius of warped reality
- **Reality-warping effects observed:**
  - Grass blades crystallizing into golden structures, shattering in wind
  - Tree bark flowing like liquid, refreezing in twisted patterns
  - Atmospheric shimmer (heat-mirage effect despite 20°C temperature)
  - Temporal distortion (falling leaves frozen mid-air for seconds, then accelerating to ground)
  - Sound distortion (bird calls echoing 3-4 times, overlapping)
- **Warden Kaelen assessment:** "Advanced corruption. Estimate 4-6 months until terminal burnout. Immediate threat. Authorization: lethal force."

**T+00:15 (Hour 14:15) — Engagement Initiation**

- Interdiction Team spread to 4 compass positions (N/S/E/W, 60m radius from target)
- **Tactic:** Coordinated ranged attack using crystal-tipped arrows (anti-Blight rune inscriptions: Thurisaz ᚦ for disruption)
- **First volley:** 4 arrows, simultaneous release
- **Target response:** Mirael laughed, gestured with hand—arrows slowed mid-flight (temporal manipulation), fell harmlessly 10m short
- **Warden Kaelen (via signal):** "Close-range assault required. Prepare for casualties."

**T+00:17 (Hour 14:17) — Close-Range Assault**

- 2 operatives (East and West positions) charged Mirael simultaneously (split attention tactic)
- **Mirael's counter:** Extended both hands—hyper-genesis wave radiated outward
- **Effect on East operative (Operative Lyssa Windrunner):**
  - Caught by hyper-genesis wave at 15m distance
  - Body underwent catastrophic mutation: limbs elongated 30cm in 2 seconds, skin split (golden blood), screaming transformed to gurgling
  - Terminal within 8 seconds; body collapsed, continued mutating post-mortem
  - Warden Kaelen: "Lyssa down. Continue assault."
- **West operative (Operative Talon Swiftblade)** evaded wave via canopy leap (6m vertical jump, Ljósálfar agility)
  - Landed 8m from Mirael, threw crystal dagger (Thurisaz ᚦ inscription)
  - Dagger struck Mirael's shoulder—reality-warping disrupted momentarily (atmospheric shimmer cleared for 2 seconds)
  - Mirael screamed: "You don't understand! The Blight is singing! It's BEAUTIFUL!"

**T+00:18 (Hour 14:18) — Critical Engagement**

- **Warden Kaelen (North position)** charged during disruption window
- **Armed:** Crystal spear (dual inscription: Thurisaz ᚦ disruption + Isa ᛁ binding)

- **Warden thrust spear through Mirael's chest** (precise strike, heart penetration)
- **Effect:** Disruption rune activated—reality-warping collapsed violently inward
  - Mirael's body convulsed, eyes bleeding violet light
  - Hyper-genesis wave reversed: Mirael's own flesh crystallized, then shattered
  - Death: Instantaneous; body disintegrated into golden dust
  - Shockwave: 40m radius vegetation flash-growth (3-5m surge), then immediate death (plants turned black, crumbled)
