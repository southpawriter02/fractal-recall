## I. Geographic Overview & Access

### 1.1 Location and Topography

- **Realm:** Vanaheim (Bio-Engineering Deck)
- **Specific Location:** Suspended in the Gloom-Veil (The Mid-Fall), a perpetually misty void between canopy and forest floor
- **Construction:** Series of modular bio-domes and containment pens suspended by colossal adamantium-weave cables
- **Altitude:** Approximately 800-1,200 meters below canopy level
- **Access Points:** Single retractable sky-bridge (Grove-Clan controlled, requires Council authorization), emergency rappel points from canopy (unmaintained, 30-40% cable degradation risk)
- **Coordinates:** No fixed GPS due to Gloom-Veil psychic interference; navigation via Grove-Clan warden guides using bio-luminescent waypoint markers
- **Visibility Range:** 15-30 meters in typical spore-fog conditions; zero during Gene-Storm bloom surges

### 1.2 Environmental Conditions

- **Atmosphere:** Breathable but toxic - O₂ 19-20%, elevated H₂S (6-12 ppm), psychoactive fungal spore load
- **Temperature:** 18-24°C year-round (bio-thermal regulation from canopy above)
- **Humidity:** 85-95% constant (condensation drip from superstructure creates perpetual "rain")
- **Lighting:** Perpetual twilight; bioluminescent flora provide 0.3-1.2 lux ambient illumination
- **Acoustic Profile:** Constant low-frequency hum (corrupted containment field resonance at 18-22 Hz); overlaid with sporadic animal calls, metal stress groans, Data-Wraith whisper-static

### 1.3 Structural Integrity Assessment

- **Cable Integrity:** Primary support cables show 15-25% strand degradation; secondary stabilizers at 40-60% nominal strength
- **Dome Seals:** Bio-organic membrane seals operating at 30-50% containment effectiveness (persistent breaches allowing organism escape/ingress)
- **Power Systems:** Emergency geothermal taps provide 8-15% of original facility power load; lighting, environmental control, and mag-locks intermittent
- **Catastrophic Collapse Risk:** Estimated 2-4% annual probability; Grove-Clan monitors but lacks engineering capacity for repairs

## II. Pre-Glitch Function: Project Fenrir Arsenal

### 2.1 Original Purpose and Strategic Context

The Gleipnir Pens were constructed during Cycle 947-952 (approximately 100 years pre-Glitch) as the Vanir Collective's answer to the Aesir Einherjar program. While the Aesir focused on human-piloted war machines, the Vanir pursued bio-engineered apex predators as strategic deterrent weapons.

**Design Philosophy:**
- Leverage Vanir bio-manipulation superiority to create organisms optimized for specific combat roles
- Eliminate pilot dependency (and associated Argentum-Nexus vulnerabilities)
- Produce self-replicating weapon systems requiring minimal logistical support
- Maintain plausible deniability through "ecological accident" cover narratives

**Project Fenrir Objectives:**
1. Develop 8-12 apex predator genotypes optimized for different theaters (aerial, aquatic, subterranean, urban)
2. Engineer behavioral constraints preventing indiscriminate aggression toward Vanir personnel
3. Establish breeding populations for rapid deployment scaling
4. Create containment infrastructure for long-term weapons stockpiling

### 2.2 Fenrir-Pattern Specifications

The "Fenrir-Pattern" designation refers to the apex genotype - a 6-8 meter quadrupedal predator combining wolf anatomy with enhanced musculature, armored dermal plating, and bio-Aetheric sensory organs.

**Combat Capabilities (Pre-Glitch Documentation):**
- **Bite Force:** 18,000-24,000 N (sufficient to shear Adamant-Weave cables)
- **Sprint Speed:** 85-110 km/h over short distances (200-400m)
- **Endurance:** Can maintain 40-50 km/h pursuit for 6-8 hours
- **Sensory Range:** Thermal detection 2-3 km; Aetheric signature tracking 800-1,200m; olfactory tracking 15-20 km downwind
- **Pack Coordination:** Neural-link network enables 8-12 individual coordination without external command structure

**Behavioral Constraints (Engineered Inhibitions):**
- **Vanir Recognition Protocols:** Bio-electric signature recognition preventing attacks on authorized personnel (95%+ reliability)
- **Territorial Anchoring:** Genetically imprinted territorial boundaries limiting operational range to 20-30 km radius from designated "den"
- **Starvation Docility:** Extended food deprivation (14+ days) triggers metabolic dormancy rather than hunting desperation

## III. The Ginnungagap Catastrophe: Containment Breach

### 3.1 H-Hour Event Sequence

When the Ginnungagap Glitch struck, the Gleipnir Pens experienced cascading system failures:

**H+00:00 to H+00:30 - Initial Cascade:**
- Runic inhibitor inscriptions corrupted by Blight propagation wave
- Behavioral constraint protocols dissolve; specimens experience sudden release from engineered docility
- Atmospheric sedation systems fail; specimens achieve 100% alertness simultaneously
- Neural-link coordination network floods with paradox data; pack hierarchy collapses into chaotic aggression

**H+00:30 to H+02:00 - Containment Failure:**
- Bio-membrane walls lose integrity as Runic anchors fail
- Magnetic floor plates deactivate; specimens approach walls without inhibition
- First breaches occur in Pens 7, 11, and 14 (closest to Genesis Engine corruption zone)
- Escaped specimens attack adjacent pens from exterior, accelerating additional breaches

**H+02:00 to H+06:00 - Massacre of Researchers:**
- Station personnel (estimated 80-120 individuals) attempt evacuation via sky-bridge
- Fenrir-Pattern specimens no longer recognize Vanir bio-electric signatures
- 95%+ casualty rate; survivors who reached canopy reported "systematic hunting" behavior
- Emergency cryo-flood protocols activated by lone surviving engineer (partial success: 30-40% specimens entered hibernation)

**H+06:00 to H+24:00 - Ecological Dispersal:**
- Non-hibernating specimens (estimated 40-60 individuals across all genotypes) disperse into Vanaheim wilderness
- Breeding populations establish in Under-growth and Gloom-Veil territories
- Contemporary Fenrir-derived creatures (Chasm-Crawlers, Root-Vipers, proto-beasts) descend from these escapees

## IV. Contemporary Hazard Environment

### 4.1 Resident Threats

**Hibernating Fenrir-Patterns (Estimated 12-18 Specimens):**
- Locations: Pens 3, 5, 9, 12 (cryo-flood systems partially functional)
- Status: Metabolic dormancy; body temperature 8-12°C, respiration 1-2 breaths/minute
- Activation Risk: Power fluctuations, thermal disruption, acoustic shock can trigger awakening (15-30 minute transition to full combat readiness)
- Threat Assessment: Tier A+ hazard if awakened; 200m stand-off minimum, immediate evacuation protocols

**Data-Wraith of Arinbjorn (Unique Entity):**
- Identity: Dr. Arinbjorn Wolfkeeper, Project Fenrir lead geneticist
- Manifestation: Psychic echo trapped in facility observation galleries
- Behavior: Broadcasts fragmented memories of specimen care routines, ethical debates, final moments during breach
- Hazard: Type Delta CPS accumulation; exposure >20 minutes induces empathetic identification with researcher's guilt/terror
- Phenomenon: Unlike typical Data-Wraiths (simple looping), Arinbjorn demonstrates context-responsive communication suggesting partial consciousness retention

**Proto-Beasts (Gleipnir Hounds):**
- Population: 6-10 individuals using Pens as territorial den
- Behavior: Treat facility as ancestral territory; aggressive toward intruders but do not pursue beyond 400m perimeter
- Pack Dynamics: Retain neural-link coordination (corrupted); vocalizations include ultrasonic components causing disorientation
- Hunting Pattern: Ambush tactics using structural cover; coordinate pincer attacks with 80%+ success rate against unaware targets

## V. Strategic Value and Research Objectives

### 5.1 Genetic Archive Recovery

The Pens contain irreplaceable pre-Glitch bio-engineering documentation:

**Target Materials:**
- **Genetic Sequencing Libraries:** Digital archives storing Fenrir-Pattern baseline genomes (uncorrupted blueprints)
- **Behavioral Modification Protocols:** Documentation of neural-link network architecture, inhibition inscription formulas
- **Comparative Mutagenesis Data:** Pre-Glitch vs. post-Glitch specimen genetics enabling Blight-mutation reverse-engineering
- **Project Fenrir Development Logs:** Ethical debates, failed genotypes, weaponization doctrine

**Access Challenges:**
- Primary data storage in sealed research wing (Observation Complex Gamma)
- Access route through three breached domes, across two collapsed catwalks, past hibernating Fenrir den
- Data format likely incompatible with contemporary systems (requires specialist data recovery equipment)

**Estimated Recovery Timeline:**
- Optimistic: 8-12 expeditions over 18-24 months
- Realistic: 15-20 expeditions over 36-48 months
- Pessimistic: Impossible without Grove-Clan military-scale support operation

### 5.2 Ethical Research Constraints

Jötun-Reader doctrine prohibits deliberate Depth-Sickness induction studies, limiting research to observational data from operational expeditions. This creates knowledge gaps about Stage 3 progression mechanisms and long-term neurological effects of repeated severe episodes.

## VI. Factional Perspectives

### 6.1 Grove-Clans: Shameful Legacy and Protective Custody

The Grove-Clans regard the Pens as a monument to Vanir hubris and moral failure.

**Cultural Interpretation:**
- Project Fenrir violated core Vanir philosophy: "That which grows must serve life, not death"
- Weaponization of bio-engineering represented ideological capitulation to Aesir militarism
- Contemporary Grove-Clans see themselves as custodians preventing legacy horrors from spreading

**Territorial Control:**
- Druidic Council maintains sky-bridge access monopoly
- Unauthorized expeditions treated as trespass (subject to arrest, equipment confiscation)
- Permits granted only after rigorous ethical review (8-12 week approval timeline)

**Strategic Stance:**
- Prefer facility remain sealed/forgotten rather than exploited
- Will authorize study if expedition demonstrates: (1) genuine research intent, (2) no specimen extraction, (3) risk containment protocols, (4) findings-sharing agreement

### 6.2 Jötun-Readers: Archive Recovery Priority

The Scriptorium Division of Applied Biology views the Pens as a critical research target.

**Research Priorities:**
1. Genetic archive recovery (baseline Fenrir genomes, modification protocols)
2. Comparative mutagenesis study (pre-Glitch vs. proto-beast lineages)
3. Runic inhibitor failure analysis (Blight propagation mechanics)
4. Historical documentation (Aesir-Vanir weapons development context)

**Operational Doctrine:**
- Non-invasive methodology: Minimize structural disturbance, avoid specimen awakening
- Ethical restraint: No live specimen extraction, observe Grove-Clan territorial sovereignty
- Long-term engagement: Accept 3-5 year timeline for comprehensive study
- Partnership cultivation: Demonstrate trustworthiness to Grove-Clan Council

### 6.3 Iron-Banes: Extermination Imperative

The Iron-Bane Crusade views the Pens as a demonic weapons forge requiring purification.

**Theological Position:**
- Fenrir-Patterns are "demon-spawn" - Blight-corrupted abominations defiling natural order
- Hibernating specimens must be destroyed before awakening creates regional catastrophe
- Data archives should be burned to prevent "corruption knowledge" proliferation

**Strategic Reality:**
- Grove-Clan territorial control prevents direct assault
- Limited Iron-Bane technical capacity for Gloom-Veil operations
- Proposed fire-bombing strategies unlikely to succeed (high humidity, limited accelerant access)

## VII. Operational Protocols: Field Card

**BEFORE: Preparation Phase**

**Threat Assessment:**
- **Environmental:** Type Delta CPS (Arinbjorn Data-Wraith), H₂S/CO₂ pockets, spore density, structural collapse
- **Biological:** Tier A+ (hibernating Fenrir), Tier B (proto-beasts), Tier C (Gloom-Stalkers, Blight-Jellies)
- **Runic:** Failed inhibitor inscriptions broadcasting behavioral override commands

**Equipment Requirements:**
- **Climbing Gear:** Redundant harness systems, 40m rope minimum, percussion testing hammer
- **Atmospheric Monitoring:** H₂S/CO₂ detector, spore counter, O₂ reserve (4-6 hour supply)
- **CPS Protection:** Kvasir-Pattern cognitive buffers (if available), cognitive assessment questionnaire cards
- **Communication:** Grove-Clan warden escort (mandatory), signal flares (emergency recall), acoustic dampers

**DURING: Active Engagement**

**Detection Indicators:**
- **Proto-Beast Proximity:** Ultrasonic vocalizations, claw-scrape acoustic signature, pack coordination howls
- **Structural Failure Warning:** Increased cable hum pitch, metallic stress groans, catwalk flexion >15cm
- **Atmospheric Hazard:** H₂S detector alarm, sudden CO₂-induced headache/dizziness, visible spore cloud density
- **Runic Interference:** Compulsive approach urges, aggressive ideation, temporary dissociation

**Immediate Response Protocols:**
- **Proto-Beast Contact:** Acoustic damper deployment, defensive formation, signal flare launch, smoke bomb retreat
- **Structural Emergency:** Immediate weight transfer to secondary support points, percussion test all surfaces
- **Atmospheric Crisis:** Don emergency O₂, retreat to higher elevation, activate ventilation equipment
- **Runic Override:** Verbal reality anchoring, physical distance from inscription source, cognitive reassessment protocol

**AFTER: Disengagement Phase**

**Immediate (0-24 Hours):**
- Medical Screening: Pulmonary function test, CPS extended monitoring (24-hour observation)
- Equipment Inspection: Rope/harness integrity check, atmospheric detector calibration
- Debriefing: Grove-Clan warden report, structural integrity log update, specimen status documentation

**Outcome Statement:**
Mission success defined by: (1) Data recovery or observational objectives met, (2) Zero specimen awakening incidents, (3) Zero unauthorized structural disturbance, (4) Grove-Clan satisfaction with conduct, (5) Zero permanent team injuries
