# Harrow-Sick (Post-Glitch Degenerative Condition)

**Header Block**

- Document ID: CODX-PATH-HARROW-SICK-v1.0
- Layer: 2 (Diagnostic)
- Author (Faction): Jötun-Reader Medical Division
- Classification: Pathology • Degenerative Condition • High Incidence

### 1. Identification and context

**Headword:** Harrow-Sick — a progressive degenerative condition linked to chronic Aetheric Blight exposure

**Type:** Pathological syndrome • Multi-system degeneration • Environmental exposure disease

**Endemic to:** All post-Glitch realms, with highest incidence in Jötunheim industrial zones, Asgard ruin periphery, and Midgard frontier settlements operating near Blight-saturated terrain

**Primary characteristics:** The Harrow-Sick is a progressive condition characterized by accelerating cellular breakdown, neurological deterioration, and eventual systemic organ failure in individuals subjected to prolonged low-level Aetheric Blight exposure. Unlike acute Cognitive Paradox Syndrome (CPS), which manifests as sudden psychic trauma, the Harrow-Sick develops gradually over months to years, often escaping diagnosis until irreversible damage has occurred.

**Incidence:** Estimated 3-7% of frontier populations show clinical Harrow-Sick markers. Salvage workers, Rangers operating in Blight-adjacent zones, and Ruin-Delvers face 15-25% lifetime incidence rates. Urban Hold populations demonstrate significantly lower rates (~0.5%).

### 2. Symptomology and progression

**Stage 1 — Onset (months 1-6): "The Dimming"**

Initial symptoms are subtle and frequently dismissed as fatigue or seasonal malaise:

- **Chrono-dysphoria:** Distorted time perception; minutes feel compressed or elongated without external cause
- **Peripheral numbness:** Loss of sensation beginning in fingertips and toes, progressing proximally
- **Sleep architecture disruption:** Inability to achieve deep sleep; vivid, repetitive "loop-dreams" featuring fragmented pre-Glitch imagery
- **Appetite changes:** Metallic taste perception; aversion to high-iron foods

**Stage 2 — Progression (months 6-18): "The Harrowing"**

Named symptoms emerge that distinguish the condition from general Blight exposure fatigue:

- **Dermal iron deposits:** Subcutaneous metallic nodules develop along meridian lines, visible as dark striations beneath the skin. These deposits test positive for corrupted Aetheric resonance but differ from Iron Husk formations observed in Svin-fylking
- **Cognitive fragmentation:** Short-term memory degradation; difficulty forming new procedural memories. Patients describe "losing time" — performing routine tasks with no subsequent recollection
- **Proprioceptive drift:** Progressive loss of body-schema awareness; patients report limbs feeling "distant" or "belonging to someone else"
- **Pain amplification:** Hyperalgesia develops without apparent nociceptive cause. Light touch perceived as burning; ambient sound registers as physically painful

**Stage 3 — Terminal (months 18-36): "The Hollowing"**

Advanced Harrow-Sick presents unmistakable clinical features:

- **Motor dissolution:** Voluntary movement becomes progressively uncoordinated; fine motor control lost entirely
- **Identity erosion:** Patients lose ability to recognize familiar faces, locations, and eventually their own reflection. Described as "watching yourself become a stranger"
- **Aetheric resonance cascade:** The subcutaneous iron deposits begin generating measurable Aetheric field distortions, creating localized micro-Blight zones around the patient
- **Systemic failure:** Multi-organ shutdown proceeding from peripheral to central systems

**Prognosis:** Without intervention, Stage 3 patients survive 2-6 months. No documented cases of spontaneous recovery from Stage 2 or beyond. Stage 1, if identified, shows ~40% stabilization rate with immediate Blight exposure cessation — but not reversal.

### 3. Proposed etiologies

**Diagnostic consensus remains incomplete.** Three competing models dominate current medical debate:

**Model A — Cumulative Blight Saturation (Jötun-Reader dominant model):**

Chronic low-level Aetheric Blight exposure exceeds cellular repair capacity. Corrupted Aetheric particles accumulate in tissues with high metabolic activity (neural, hepatic, renal), progressively disrupting cellular signaling. The iron deposits represent the body's failed attempt to sequester corrupted particles — biological containment that ultimately fails as deposit volume exceeds tissue tolerance.

**Model B — Resonance Cascade Hypothesis:**

The Harrow-Sick represents not cumulative damage but a threshold phenomenon. Individual Aetheric tolerance varies; when exposure crosses a patient-specific threshold, a self-reinforcing feedback loop initiates — corrupted Aether generates corrupted cellular products, which in turn amplify local Aetheric distortion. This model explains the sudden acceleration from Stage 1 to Stage 2 observed in many patients.

**Model C — Void-Marked Spectrum Theory:**

The most controversial proposal suggests the Harrow-Sick and the Void-Marked condition exist on a shared pathological spectrum. Both involve Aetheric exposure producing physical markers and neurological changes. In this model, the Void-Marked represent individuals whose physiology adapted to Aetheric saturation, while Harrow-Sick patients are those whose bodies reject the same process. The divergent outcomes may be genetically determined.

> _"The Void-Marked walk with the Blight inside them. The Harrow-Sick die from it. The question that haunts us: what decides which path a body takes?"_

### 4. Relationship to Void-Marked condition

The connection between Harrow-Sick and Void-Marked status remains one of the most significant unsolved medical questions in post-Glitch pathology.

**Observed correlations:**

- Void-Marked individuals show complete immunity to Harrow-Sick — no documented cases of dual manifestation
- Early Stage 1 Harrow-Sick patients occasionally undergo "conversion events" where symptoms resolve and Void-Marked physical markers appear instead — estimated frequency: 1 in 200 cases
- Blood serum analysis reveals inverted Aetheric resonance signatures between the two conditions: Harrow-Sick patients show chaotic, high-frequency signatures while Void-Marked show stable, low-frequency patterns
- Family clusters demonstrate mixed outcomes: siblings with identical exposure histories may develop opposite conditions

**Research limitation:** Ethical constraints prohibit deliberate Blight exposure studies. All data derives from retrospective analysis of natural exposure populations.

### 5. Treatment and management

**No curative treatment exists.** Current medical practice focuses on symptom management and exposure reduction:

**Stage 1 interventions:**

- Immediate and complete removal from Blight-adjacent environments
- Dvergr-crafted resonance dampening amulets (reduces ambient Aetheric exposure by ~60%)
- Herbal preparations: Ash-Root tincture (anti-inflammatory), Frost-Moss poultice (neural stabilization) — palliative only
- Monitored rest in Hearth-Hold sanctuaries with known low-Blight readings

**Stage 2 interventions:**

- Iron deposit management: Careful surgical excision of accessible nodules, though regrowth occurs within 2-4 months
- Cognitive support: Structured routine protocols and companion assignment to mitigate memory loss impact
- Pain management: Dvergr-distilled numbing compounds (topical and systemic)
- Aetheric shielding: Experimental containment garments using Weir-Bone filaments to create personal Blight barriers

**Stage 3 interventions:**

- Palliative care only. Focus on comfort and dignity
- Aetheric isolation: Patients housed in shielded chambers to prevent micro-Blight radiation affecting caregivers
- Cultural rites vary by faction (see §7)

### 6. Field Card — Harrow-Sick identification protocol

**CRITICAL DOCTRINE:** Early identification is the only reliable intervention. All field teams must screen for Stage 1 markers during routine medical assessments.

**SCREENING — Quarterly assessment for exposed personnel**

- **OWNER:** Medical officer or senior Ranger conducts assessment; Incident Commander authorizes operational stand-down if Stage 1 indicators detected
- **Assessment battery:**
  - **Chrono-perception test:** Timed interval estimation (>20% deviation = flag)
  - **Peripheral sensation mapping:** Standardized pinprick/thermal test on extremities
  - **Sleep journal review:** Pattern analysis for loop-dream content and frequency
  - **Dermal inspection:** Full-body visual examination for subcutaneous discolouration or nodule formation
- **Positive result protocol:** Immediate removal from Blight-adjacent duties; referral to Jötun-Reader Medical Division for advanced diagnostic workup

### 7. Factional perspectives

**Jötun-Readers — Diagnostic research and ethical constraints**

The Medical Division maintains the most comprehensive Harrow-Sick case registry, documenting over 3,400 confirmed cases across 783 years. Research emphasis on developing predictive biomarkers for pre-Stage 1 susceptibility screening. Ethical debate continues regarding the permissibility of studying Void-Marked conversion events — does observation constitute passive experimentation on suffering patients?

**Iron-Banes — The Blight's Harvest**

Iron-Bane theology interprets Harrow-Sick as direct evidence that all Aetheric technology is fundamentally destructive. The condition serves as a powerful recruitment tool: _"This is what the machines do to flesh. This is why we must purge the iron from the earth."_ Crusade-controlled Holds provide charitable hospice care for terminal patients — genuine compassion weaponized as theological propaganda.

**Hearth-Clans — The Wasting**

Rural Holds call the condition "The Wasting" and regard it with superstitious dread. Folk remedies (copper coin poultices, salt circles, specific prayer formulae) remain widely practiced despite zero efficacy. Some Holds exile Stage 2 patients, fearing the micro-Blight emanations will contaminate crops and livestock.

**God-Sleepers — The Forge-Price**

God-Sleeper doctrine frames Harrow-Sick as a trial imposed by the sleeping Forge-Gods — a test of faith and bodily endurance. Pilgrims who develop the condition during sacred journeys are venerated as "Forge-Touched." This framing, while psychologically supportive, discourages patients from seeking medical treatment during the critical Stage 1 window.

**Dvergr — Pragmatic metallurgical interest**

Guild engineers study the iron deposits purely as material science curiosities: what process creates metallic structures within living tissue? The Dvergr have contributed the most effective shielding technologies (resonance dampening amulets, Weir-Bone containment garments) but express limited interest in the pathology itself.
