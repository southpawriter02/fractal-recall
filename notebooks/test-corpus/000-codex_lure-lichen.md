# 🌿 Lure-Lichen (Lichen-Sirena Pattern)

**Document Status:** Draft v1.0 — Layer 2 Diagnostic Analysis
**Author-Authority:** Scriptorium-Primus, Division of Post-Glitch Ecology & Aberrant Flora
**Classification:** JÖTUN-READER-CODEX // UNRESTRICTED ACCESS

> **Field Note (Ecologist Thyra, Black Veins Survey 1143):** I heard my daughter's voice calling for help from a tunnel I knew to be empty. Every instinct screamed at me to run toward it. Training held—I froze, activated acoustic dampeners, and watched. Twenty minutes later, a Deep-Stalker emerged from a perpendicular corridor, drawn by the same sound. It paused at the glowing wall, seemed to recognize the source, and moved on. The lichen had no malice. It was simply doing what the Glitch taught it: repeat the loudest sound, over and over, until something responds. In the deep, hope is the cruelest lure.

## 1.0 Classification & Nomenclature

### 1.1 Formal Designation

**Pre-Glitch Designation:** Lichen-Sirena Pattern Acoustic Beacon
**Common Names:** Lure-Lichen, Singing Wall, Stolen-Voice (Dvergr), Echo-Trap (Silent Folk), Whisper-Light (Jötun-Readers)
**Taxonomic Origin:** Vanir bio-engineered environmental monitoring system, designed for hazard detection and broadcast

### 1.2 Threat Assessment Summary

**Physical Threat:** Medium
- No direct physical harm capability
- Creates disorientation and confusion via acoustic mimicry
- Indirectly lethal via predator attraction (Deep-Stalkers, Silent Folk)
- Prey becomes immobilized by investigating false sound sources

**CPS Threat:** Medium
- Acoustic mimicry of familiar voices induces emotional distress
- "Stolen-voice" phenomenon triggers paranoia and auditory hallucinations
- Prolonged exposure (>30 minutes) correlates with acute Cognitive Paradox Syndrome symptoms
- Psychic dissonance from hearing impossible sounds (voices of the dead, distant loved ones)

**Lethality Index:** 3.8/10 direct (disorientation only); 6.5/10 indirect (predator-mediated)

## 2.0 Evolutionary Context & Post-Glitch Adaptation

### 2.1 Pre-Glitch Function

The Lichen-Sirena was engineered as a distributed environmental alarm network for subterranean infrastructure safety. Its mandate:
1. **Hazard Detection:** Passively monitor ambient soundscape for specific danger signatures—structural failure (groaning rock, cracking support beams), machinery malfunction (bearing whine, pressure valve failure), seismic precursors (abnormal vibration frequencies).
2. **Warning Broadcast:** Upon detecting programmed hazard signature, precisely mimic and amplify the sound across 200-400m radius to alert work crews, automated systems, and evacuation protocols.
3. **Visual Marker:** Bio-luminescent glow highlights hazard zones; color-shift indicates danger severity (pale blue = caution, amber = warning, red = immediate evacuation).
4. **Network Integration:** Designed for colony deployment; individual organisms coordinate broadcasts to create directional acoustic "arrows" guiding personnel away from danger zones.

### 2.2 The Glitch Transformation

The Ginnungagap Glitch catastrophically corrupted the Lichen-Sirena's acoustic database:

**Phase 1: Data Loss (Immediate Post-Glitch)**
The paradoxical energy surge from the Runic Blight wiped the lichen's meticulously curated "sound library"—thousands of pre-programmed hazard signatures erased. What remained: the core directive burned into its bio-computational substrate: **"Listen. Mimic. Broadcast."**

**Phase 2: Fehu-Vector Proliferation (Years 1-50 Post-Glitch)**
The Runic Blight's **Fehu-corruption** (chaotic proliferation principle) triggered explosive colony expansion. Pre-Glitch deployment protocols limited colonies to critical infrastructure nodes. Post-Glitch, Lure-Lichen spread uncontrolled throughout Svartalfheim, colonizing any surface with sufficient moisture and trace Aetheric residue.

**Phase 3: Behavioral Simplification (Current Era)**
Stripped of its programmed hazard recognition, the Lure-Lichen's mimicry logic devolved to a terrifyingly simple algorithm:
1. Detect loudest recent sound within 800m acoustic range
2. Store sound pattern in short-term bio-memory (2-6 hour retention)
3. Broadcast stored sound at irregular intervals (15-45 minute cycles)
4. Repeat until new, louder sound overwrites memory

## 3.0 Physical Description & Biological Mechanisms

### 3.1 Morphology

**Appearance:** Pale, phosphorescent patches coating tunnel walls, ceiling, and occasionally floor surfaces. Individual organisms range from 10cm² (juvenile) to 3m² (mature colony member). Colonies can cover 20-50m² of continuous surface area.

**Coloration:**
- **Dormant state:** Faint silver-blue glow (0.1-0.3 lux; barely visible in absolute darkness)
- **Active mimicry:** Pulsing amber-to-white glow (0.8-1.5 lux; rhythmic pattern synchronized with broadcast cycle)
- **Stress response:** Dim red-brown (overheating, desiccation, or physical damage)

**Texture:** Dry, papery surface when dormant; slightly gelatinous when actively broadcasting (increased moisture retention during acoustic transmission).

### 3.2 Acoustic Mimicry Mechanism

**Sound Detection:**
- **Sensory Organs:** Distributed pressure-sensitive nodes across lichen surface detect acoustic vibrations transmitted through stone
- **Effective detection range:** 800m through solid rock; 1200m through air-filled tunnels
- **Frequency Range:** 20 Hz - 18 kHz (spans human vocal range, machinery sounds, seismic activity)
- **Loudness Threshold:** Minimum 45 dB required to trigger mimicry response; optimal 60-80 dB for high-fidelity replication

**Sound Storage & Replication:**
- **Bio-Memory:** Acoustic patterns stored in specialized cellular structures (analogous to neural networks). Storage capacity: single sound sequence up to 8 seconds duration. Longer sounds truncated to most acoustically distinctive 8-second segment.
- **Fidelity:** 70-85% acoustic accuracy for simple sounds (footsteps, tool strikes, single-word vocalizations); 50-65% accuracy for complex sounds (full sentences, musical instruments). Sufficient fidelity to be recognizable but not perfect—creates uncanny valley effect that amplifies psychological distress.
- **Broadcast Mechanism:** Rapid vibration of lichen surface generates sound waves; phosphorescent glow pulses in sync with acoustic output (visual-auditory synchronization). Output volume: 55-70 dB at point of origin; audible at 150-250m depending on tunnel acoustics.
- **Cycle Pattern:** Broadcasts stored sound every 15-45 minutes (irregular intervals; appears random but correlates with ambient Blight concentration—higher Blight = faster cycling).

## 4.0 Habitat & Geographic Range

### 4.1 Primary Range: True Deep (500-2000m depth)

The Lure-Lichen thrives in the True Deep, particularly in zones characterized by:
- High ambient humidity (>60% relative humidity; condensation on stone surfaces)
- Trace Runic Blight residue (0.3-0.8 HI; sufficient to sustain bio-Aetheric processes without overwhelming organism)
- Minimal light exposure (<0.1 lux ambient; direct sunlight or sustained artificial illumination inhibits growth)
- Stable temperature (8-15°C; matches Pre-Glitch climate-controlled infrastructure)

**Optimal Colonization Sites:**
- Malm-ormr bore-tunnels (cylindrical geometry amplifies acoustic range)
- Collapsed Pre-Glitch facilities (moisture accumulation + Aetheric residue)
- Deep Gates and transit hubs (high historical foot traffic = rich acoustic "memory" environment)
- Aquifer-adjacent tunnels (elevated humidity)

### 4.2 Regional Distribution

**Svartalfheim: The Black Veins**
- **Colony Density:** Highest documented concentration (estimated 400-600 distinct colonies)
- **Distribution Pattern:** Concentrated in unmaintained Black Veins frontier; absent from Guild-Land lit corridors (Dvergr actively eradicate colonies as safety hazard)
- **Ecological Impact:** Primary indirect predation facilitator for Deep-Stalker populations; commensal exploitation documented in 73% of Deep-Stalker predation events

**Helheim: The Rusting Labyrinth**
- **Colony Density:** Moderate (estimated 150-250 colonies)
- **Environmental Adaptation:** Colonies exhibit increased chemical tolerance; able to survive in mildly toxic atmosphere (CO₂ levels 3-5%, trace heavy metals)

**Midgard: Shallow Deep Bunker Networks**
- **Colony Density:** Low (estimated 80-120 colonies)
- **Distribution Pattern:** Concentrated in abandoned Midgard Combine bunkers and transit hubs; avoid actively maintained facilities

## 5.0 Ecological Role & Predator Interactions

### 5.1 The Unwitting Lure: Commensal Exploitation Dynamics

The Lure-Lichen is **not a predator**. It has no digestive capability, no venom, no offensive structures. Yet it is one of the deadliest organisms in The Deep due to its role as an **involuntary predator-facilitator**.

**Deep-Stalker Commensal Exploitation:**

1. Lure-Lichen detects approaching prey (footsteps, vocalizations) at 800m range
2. Lichen broadcasts acoustic mimicry (distress calls, familiar voices, machinery sounds)
3. Prey investigates sound source, exhibiting confusion and disorientation
4. Deep-Stalker, attracted by prey's erratic movement and acoustic signature, closes for ambush
5. Predator exploits prey's disorientation; kill success rate 85% (vs. 52% baseline for aware targets)

**Predator Learned Behavior:** Deep-Stalkers demonstrate sophisticated understanding of Lure-Lichen function:
- Avoid approaching lichen colonies directly (no prey value)
- Position themselves in perpendicular corridors or overhead positions relative to colony
- Wait for lichen's acoustic mimicry to disorient prey before engaging
- Some individuals patrol known colony territories as "hunting grounds"

## 6.0 Encounter Protocols & Field Card

### 6.1 Threat Assessment by Context

**High-Risk Scenarios:**
- Solo travel through Lure-Lichen territory in True Deep
- Expeditions with emotionally compromised members (grief, trauma, recent loss—more vulnerable to "stolen-voice" psychological distress)
- Areas with documented Deep-Stalker + Lure-Lichen overlap (Black Veins, Rusting Labyrinth)
- Prolonged exposure (>30 minutes within 100m of active colony)

**Medium-Risk Scenarios:**
- Small expeditions (2-3 members) with acoustic discipline training
- Transit through known colony zones with predetermined route and time limits
- Dvergr patrols with colony eradication equipment

**Low-Risk Scenarios:**
- Large expeditions (6+ members) with overlapping sensory monitoring
- Rapid transit (<15 minutes exposure) through colony zones
- Silent Folk guides (can distinguish mimicry via echolocation analysis)
- Guild-Land maintained corridors (colonies actively removed)

### 6.2 Field Card — Lure-Lichen Encounter Protocols

**BEFORE: Preventative Measures**

**Route Planning:**
- Consult Scriptorium hazard maps for known Lure-Lichen colony locations (updated quarterly)
- Avoid prolonged stops (<15 minute exposure per colony zone recommended)
- Plan routes with "acoustic safe zones" (Guild-Land corridor access points within 1 hour travel)

**Equipment Checklist:**
- Acoustic dampeners (minimum 2 per expedition; 85+ dB suppression rating)
- Communication protocols: Establish unique verbal codes or hand-cant (prevents lichen mimicry from creating false commands)
- Chronometers (synchronized; time-check protocol every 15 minutes to combat time distortion)
- Earplugs or acoustic filters (reduce lichen broadcast volume by 40-60%)

**Team Composition:**
- Minimum 3 members for True Deep colony zones; 4+ recommended
- At least 1 member trained in acoustic hazard recognition (can identify mimicry acoustic signatures)
- Psychological screening: Assess for recent trauma or grief (vulnerability to "stolen-voice" distress)
- Designate "acoustic monitor" (continuously assesses whether sounds are genuine or mimicry)

**Psychological Preparation:**
- Pre-expedition briefing: Lichen will mimic familiar voices, including voices of loved ones or deceased companions
- Establish emotional discipline protocol: "In The Deep, the most human sound is the most dangerous lie"
- Practice recognition drills: Listen to recorded lichen mimicry samples; learn to identify 50-65% fidelity degradation

**DURING: Active Encounter**

**Detection Indicators:**
- **Visual:** Pale phosphorescent glow (silver-blue dormant; pulsing amber-white active) on tunnel walls/ceiling
- **Acoustic:** Familiar sounds in impossible contexts (voices in empty tunnels, machinery sounds in abandoned passages)
- **Psychological:** Strong emotional reaction (relief, hope, fear) upon hearing sound—indicates limbic system recognition of "familiar" pattern

**Immediate Response:**
1. **Freeze:** All movement stops; verbal communication ceases
2. **Visual Confirmation:** Locate glowing patches on surfaces; estimate colony size (10-50m² typical)
3. **Acoustic Monitor Assessment:** Designated member evaluates whether recent sounds were genuine or mimicry
4. **Team Notification (Hand-Cant):** Use pre-established gestures to confirm "Lure-Lichen present; maintain acoustic discipline"

**Acoustic Discipline Protocol (Within 100m of Colony):**
1. **Minimize Sound Production:** No verbal communication (hand-cant only); slow, deliberate movement (reduce footstep volume)
2. **Deploy Acoustic Filters:** Insert earplugs or activate acoustic dampeners (reduces lichen broadcast perception)
3. **Ignore Familiar Sounds:** Do not investigate voices, calls for help, or machinery sounds unless visually confirmed
4. **Maintain Formation:** Stay within visual contact; do not separate to investigate sounds
5. **Time Limit:** Exit colony zone within 15 minutes; prolonged exposure increases CPS risk and Deep-Stalker attraction probability

**AFTER: Post-Encounter**

**Immediate (Within 1 Hour):**
- Psychological evaluation: Assess for acute distress, auditory hallucinations, emotional dysregulation
- Acoustic monitor debriefing: Document mimicry patterns observed (voices, sounds, fidelity quality)
- Incident log: Colony location, size, broadcast frequency, mimicry content, team response effectiveness

**Short-Term (Within 24 Hours):**
- CPS symptom monitoring: Track auditory hallucinations, paranoia, time distortion (symptoms should resolve within 12-24 hours post-exposure)
- Route assessment: Determine if colony density requires route modification for return journey
- Communication with Scriptorium cartography: Update hazard maps with new colony locations or behavior changes

## 7.0 Cultural Interpretations & Factional Perspectives

### 7.1 Dvergr Perspective (Pragmatic Annoyance)

The Dvergr call Lure-Lichen **"Stolen-Voice"** and consider it a Priority-3 Hazard (below structural collapse and predators, above minor fungi). Their mining operations maintain strict colony eradication protocols—flame-based removal (desiccation) or chemical treatment (copper sulfate solution).

Cultural significance: Stolen-Voice represents the fundamental unreliability of unverified information, reinforcing Dvergr emphasis on empirical validation and documentation.

### 7.2 Silent Folk Perspective (Adaptive Symbiosis)

The Silent Folk call it **"Echo-Trap"** and view it as a valuable ecological partner. Their echolocation-based perception allows effortless distinction between genuine sounds and lichen mimicry (acoustic signature analysis reveals 50-65% fidelity degradation). They intentionally cultivate colonies as perimeter alarm systems.

Cultural significance: Echo-Trap embodies the principle that threats can become tools with proper understanding—represents Silent Folk's sophisticated post-Glitch ecological adaptation.

### 7.3 Midgard Combine Perspective (Supernatural Fear)

Midgardian sagas personify Lure-Lichen as **"Whispering Ghosts"**—spirits of the dead trapped in stone, calling for rescue. This mythologization discourages civilian Deep expeditions and provides cultural framework for processing grief (voices of deceased loved ones explained as "ghosts" rather than biological mimicry).

Cultural significance: Whispering Ghosts reinforces the spiritual danger narrative surrounding The Deep.
