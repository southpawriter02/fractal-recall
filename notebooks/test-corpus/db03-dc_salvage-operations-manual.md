# Salvage Operations Manual — Standard Procedures and Material Classification

## Observation Context

**Compiled by:** Jötun-Reader Archaeological Division, Salvage Assessment Bureau
**Manual revision period:** Last comprehensive update Year 770 PG; field addenda through Year 783 PG
**Methodology:** Compilation of field procedures, material classification standards, and factional operational doctrine
**Purpose:** Standardised reference for safe, productive, and ethically conducted salvage operations across all realms

## §1 — Salvage Operations Overview

Salvage — the recovery and repurposing of pre-Glitch technology, materials, and infrastructure — is the foundational economic activity of the post-Glitch era. Every faction, from the smallest Hearth-Clan Hold to the largest Scavenger Baron Dreadnought, depends on salvaged materials for survival, commerce, and technological capability. This manual codifies standard procedures applicable across factional boundaries.

**Salvage categories:**

| Category       | Description                                                                                | Value Range  | Risk Level    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------ | ------------- |
| **Mundane**    | Non-Aetheric materials (metals, polymers, structural components)                           | Low-Moderate | Low           |
| **Resonant**   | Materials with residual Aetheric properties (Iron Hearts, resonance crystals)              | High         | Moderate-High |
| **Active**     | Functional or semi-functional Aetheric systems (intact servitors, operational rune arrays) | Extreme      | Extreme       |
| **Biological** | Blight-adapted organic materials (Weir-Bone, Rune-Bear components)                         | Variable     | Variable      |
| **Restricted** | Materials subject to factional prohibition or inter-faction treaty restrictions            | N/A          | Political     |

## §2 — Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 — Pre-Entry Assessment

Before entering any salvage site, teams must conduct:

1. **Blight-level survey:** Ambient Aetheric field measurement at site perimeter. Readings above 0.6 Kveld (Dvergr resonance units) require shielded equipment and rotation protocols
2. **Structural integrity assessment:** Visual and acoustic evaluation of structural stability. Pre-Glitch infrastructure may appear sound but contain hidden void spaces, corroded supports, or gravitationally unstable configurations
3. **Fauna and automata survey:** Detection sweep for active servitors (Draugr-Pattern, Svin-fylking), Blight-adapted fauna (Ash-Vargr, Rune-Bear), and territorial faction presence
4. **Claim verification:** Check for existing Spell-Lock territorial markers (Scavenger Baron claims), Trail-Cant warning signs (Ranger advisories), or Iron-Bane purification markers

### 2.2 — Extraction Protocols

**Mundane salvage:** Standard mechanical extraction. No special precautions beyond structural safety.

**Resonant salvage:** Dedicated handling procedures. Aetheric-resonant materials must be transported in dampened containers (Weir-Bone-lined preferred, lead-composite acceptable). Personnel handling resonant salvage must limit exposure to 4 hours per rotation without dampening equipment, 8 hours with full shielding.

**Active salvage:** Specialist team required. Do NOT attempt extraction of functional Aetheric systems without Jötun-Reader or Dvergr Guild technical supervision. Incorrect handling of active systems can trigger defensive protocols, Aetheric discharge, or cascading malfunction.

**Biological salvage (Weir-Bone):** Resonance-guided hand tools only. Conventional power extraction damages Aetheric trace particle distribution. Yield assessment must account for Weir-Bone's brittle laminar structure — impact extraction destroys 60-80% of material value.

### 2.3 — Void-Marked Personnel Protocols

Void-Marked individuals possess unique abilities that enhance salvage operations:

- **Aetheric perception:** Void-Marked scouts can detect Resonant and Active salvage categories without instrumentation, reducing survey time by 40-60%
- **Blight tolerance:** Void-Marked personnel can operate in Blight-saturated environments inaccessible to baseline humans
- **Weir-Bone interaction:** Void-Marked contact with Weir-Bone produces resonance amplification that enhances detection range for adjacent deposits

**Operational guidelines:** Void-Marked team members should be integrated as scouts and assessors, not primary extraction personnel. Their Aetheric perception provides maximum value during survey and evaluation phases. Warden-Host sanctuary agreements may govern Void-Marked labour participation — verify contractual status before deployment.

## §3 — Material Classification Details

### 3.1 — Weir-Bone

**Classification:** Biological / Resonant (dual category)
**Strategic value:** Critical — primary natural substitute for pre-Glitch Aetheric crystals
**Handling:** Resonance-guided hand extraction; dampened transport containers; avoid proximity to Iron Husk material (conflicting resonance causes mutual degradation)
**Trade restrictions:** Dvergr Guild claims primary extraction rights in Nidavellir deposits. Scavenger Baron territorial claims apply to surface-accessible Jötunheim deposits. Freelance extraction is legally contested.

### 3.2 — Iron Hearts

**Classification:** Resonant / Active (assessment required)
**Strategic value:** Extreme — perpetual Aetheric power cores
**Handling:** Specialist extraction only. Active Iron Hearts generate persistent Aetheric fields. Iron-Bane doctrine classifies Iron Hearts as "Demon Hearts" — possession constitutes heresy in Crusade-controlled territory.
**Factional restrictions:** Iron-Bane embargo; God-Sleeper sacred object (handling restrictions); Dvergr Guild licensed trade only.

### 3.3 — FUTHARK Inscription Arrays

**Classification:** Active — regardless of apparent functionality status
**Strategic value:** High — operative runic sequences have military, industrial, and research applications
**Handling:** DO NOT disturb without Jötun-Reader authorisation. Intact FUTHARK arrays may retain functional command sequences that activate upon physical contact. Document location and configuration; report to Archaeological Division for specialist assessment.

## §4 — Timeline of Major Discoveries

A chronological record of salvage discoveries that significantly influenced post-Glitch development:

- **Year ~40 PG:** First Iron Heart recovery. Enabled sustained power generation for early Hold infrastructure. Transformed salvage from survival necessity to strategic resource competition
- **Year ~100 PG:** Discovery of intact Jötun-Forged megastructures in deep Jötunheim. Established Jötunheim as the primary high-value salvage domain. Stimulated God-Sleeper theological movement
- **Year ~200 PG:** First Weir-Bone deposits identified in Nidavellir. Initially classified as geological curiosity; Aetheric properties discovered decades later during Dvergr mining operations
- **Year ~400 PG:** Functional FUTHARK inscription array recovered from Asgard periphery. First successful post-Glitch activation of pre-Glitch runic technology. Established Jötun-Reader Archaeological Division's primacy in active salvage assessment
- **Year ~500 PG:** Weir-Bone reclassified as strategic material following discovery of Spell-Lock core substitution capability. Triggered resource competition between Dvergr Guild, Scavenger Barons, and Rust-Clans
- **Year ~650 PG:** Void-Marked salvage teams demonstrate superior survey efficiency. Warden-Host negotiates integration agreements allowing Void-Marked participation in multi-faction expeditions

## §5 — Factional Salvage Policies

**Jötun-Readers:** Prioritise knowledge recovery over material value. Document everything before extraction. Active systems preserved intact whenever possible. Publication and analysis take precedence over trade.

**Scavenger Barons:** Maximum material extraction for commercial trade. Territorial claim enforcement through Spell-Lock marking. Forsaken labour exploitation for bulk extraction. Value measured in trade weight, not knowledge content.

**Rust-Clans:** Pragmatic freelance salvage. No territorial claims beyond immediate operational perimeter. "The Creed of the Self" — individual salvagers retain personal finds. Loose cooperation, fierce independence.

**Iron-Banes:** Destruction-first doctrine. Crusade salvage teams are primarily demolition units — recovering mundane materials while systematically destroying Aetheric infrastructure. Active salvage is heresy; resonant salvage is grudgingly tolerated for essential trade goods.

**Dvergr Guild:** Precision extraction and craft application. Guild-certified salvage teams produce highest-quality recovered materials. Trade embargo mechanisms enforce Guild pricing. Weir-Bone extraction is a Guild specialty.

**Warden-Host:** Limited direct salvage; primarily facilitates Void-Marked participation in multi-faction teams. Sanctuary stockpiles focus on medical-grade materials (Weir-Bone shielding, resonance dampening components).
