## Diagnostic Summary

The **Warden-Host** is a paramilitary sanctuary organization dedicated to the protection of displaced, persecuted, and vulnerable populations across the post-Glitch realms. Founded approximately Year 340 PG during the Age of Walls, the Warden-Host operates a network of fortified sanctuaries providing shelter, medical care, and legal advocacy for populations excluded or endangered by factional politics — most notably the Void-Marked.

The organization occupies a unique niche: simultaneously respected for its humanitarian mission and resented for its territorial claims. Warden-Host sanctuaries are recognized as neutral ground by most factions, but this neutrality is enforced through military capability rather than diplomatic agreement. The Host maintains sufficient armed strength to defend its sanctuaries against all but the largest faction-level assaults.

This observer's analysis synthesizes field observation, intercepted administrative correspondence, and direct interviews with Host officers to construct a diagnostic portrait of an organization that has weaponized compassion into territorial sovereignty.

---

## §1 — Origins and Historical Context

The Warden-Host emerged during the Age of Walls (~Year 340 PG) when the first large-scale Void-Marked populations appeared in Midgard Hold registries. As Holds grappled with the social implications of Aetheric exposure survivors — some welcoming, others expelling — a coalition of Rangers, former Hold guardsmen, and Jötun-Reader medical personnel established the first formal sanctuary: **Grith-Hold** (from Old Norse _grið_, meaning "sanctuary" or "peace").

Grith-Hold's founding charter established three principles that remain canonical:

1. **Universal Sanctuary:** Any person seeking protection from factional persecution receives shelter regardless of origin, condition, or prior allegiance
2. **Armed Neutrality:** The Host maintains sufficient military capability to defend sanctuaries but does not engage in offensive operations or factional conflicts
3. **Self-Governance:** Sanctuaries operate under Host law, independent of local Jarl authority or factional governance

By Year 450 PG, the network had expanded to twelve sanctuaries across Midgard and the Jötunheim borderlands. Current strength (Year 783 PG): seventeen active sanctuaries, approximately 2,800 Host personnel, and an estimated 8,000 sanctuary residents.

---

## §2 — Primary Functions

**Sanctuary Operations:**

The core mission: maintaining secure, self-sustaining communities for protected populations. Sanctuaries are designed as defensible Holds with agricultural capacity, medical facilities, and artisan workshops. Residents contribute labour according to ability; no debt contracts or service obligations exist — a deliberate contrast to Scavenger Baron exploitation models.

**Void-Marked Advocacy:**

The Warden-Host is the primary institutional advocate for Void-Marked rights across all realms. Host diplomats negotiate with Hold Jarls for integration policies, challenge Iron-Bane exclusion edicts, and maintain legal records documenting persecution incidents. The Host's position: Void-Marked are fully human citizens whose Aetheric condition constitutes neither threat nor disability.

**Medical Services:**

Host sanctuaries maintain the largest concentration of Harrow-Sick treatment expertise outside the Jötun-Reader Medical Division. Harrow-Sick patients who would be exiled from conservative Holds find care within sanctuary walls. The medical programme also conducts Void-Marked health monitoring, tracking long-term outcomes for the largest cohort of stable Aetheric integration cases.

**Border Patrol and Threat Detection:**

Sanctuary perimeter security relies on integrated patrol networks combining conventional Warden patrols with Void-Marked scouts whose Aetheric perception enables early detection of Blight-Storm formation, Svin-fylking warband movements, and hostile faction approach.

---

## §3 — Echo-Cant in Warden-Host Operations

The Warden-Host has developed a unique operational relationship with acoustic communication systems inspired by — though distinct from — the Silent Folk Echo-Cant:

**Warden Signal-Cant:** A simplified coded knock-and-whistle system used for covert communication between sanctuary outposts and patrol teams. While technically unrelated to true Echo-Cant, the system was designed by a Warden linguist who studied Jötun-Reader Echo-Cant phonological analysis. The resulting Signal-Cant transmits short tactical messages across distances of 200-500 metres in terrain where visual signals fail.

**Acoustic Perimeter Defence:** Some frontier sanctuaries near Svartalfheim approaches employ passive acoustic monitoring arrays modelled on Silent Folk territorial detection principles. These systems detect ground vibration patterns consistent with approaching groups, providing 15-30 minute early warning depending on terrain.

---

## §4 — Territorial Disputes and Factional Conflicts

**Conflict with Iron-Bane Crusade:**

The most significant ongoing conflict. Iron-Bane doctrine demands the expulsion or "purification" of Void-Marked populations. Warden-Host sanctuaries sheltering Void-Marked residents directly obstruct this objective. Flashpoints include:

- **Territorial sovereignty disputes:** Iron-Bane Crusaders claim theological authority over "contaminated" populations regardless of geographic jurisdiction. The Warden-Host rejects this claim absolutely.
- **Border incidents:** Documented cases of Iron-Bane patrols attempting to remove Void-Marked individuals from sanctuary-adjacent territories. Host response is consistently military: armed escort back to sanctuary boundaries.
- **Rhetorical warfare:** Iron-Bane propaganda labels the Warden-Host as "Blight sympathisers" and "corruption harbourers." The Host counters with documented evidence of Crusade violence against civilian populations.

**Cooperation with Rangers Guild:**

A pragmatic alliance. Rangers provide intelligence on route conditions, Blight-Storm tracking, and faction movements. The Warden-Host provides medical services, resupply points, and secure communication relays. The relationship is transactional but genuinely productive for both parties.

**Hearth-Clan Relations:**

Variable. Progressive Holds view sanctuaries as valuable community infrastructure and contribute supplies. Conservative Holds resent the implicit challenge to Jarl authority — an independent, armed enclave operating under separate governance within or adjacent to Hold territory is an inherent political threat regardless of humanitarian justification.

---

## §5 — Governance Structure

**Shield-Warden (Commander):** Elected by Council of Wardens; five-year term with unlimited re-election. Current Shield-Warden: Astrid Grith-Born (third term, Year 773 PG appointment).

**Council of Wardens:** One representative per sanctuary. Responsible for policy, resource allocation, and diplomatic coordination. Meets annually at Grith-Hold; emergency sessions via messenger network.

**Sanctuary Stewards:** Individual sanctuary commanders with operational autonomy. Stewards report to Council but maintain tactical decision-making authority for local conditions.

**Residents' Assembly:** Non-binding advisory body allowing sanctuary residents to raise concerns and propose policy changes. Genuine participatory mechanism — not purely symbolic.
