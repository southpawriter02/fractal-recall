## Evidence Excerpt

### Sample Collection

- **Location:** 50m from northern shore, mid-channel flow
- **Method:** Remote drone with sealed containment vessel
- **Volume:** 500ml collected, 100ml analyzed, 400ml archived
- **Appearance:** Iridescent purple-green, viscous (consistency: motor oil)
- **Odor:** Acrid chemical burn, detectable at 20m upwind

### Chemical Composition

| Compound | Percentage | Properties |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Industrial Coolant Base** | 60% | Pre-Glitch formula IC-7 (ethylene glycol derivative); moderate toxicity |
| **Heavy Metals** | 25% | Lead, mercury, cadmium, arsenic; extreme toxicity (chronic = organ failure) |
| **Runic Blight Contamination** | 10% | Aetheric particulate suspension; accelerates Rust-Rot Moss proliferation |
| **Unknown Compound Alpha** | 3% | Novel organometallic; self-catalyzing oxidation; rapid steel corrosion |
| **Unknown Compound Beta** | 1% | Fluorescent organic molecule; bioluminescent under UV; effects unknown |
| **Particulate Debris** | 1% | Metallic dust, rubber fragments, organic matter |

### Corrosion Testing

| Material | Penetration Rate | Assessment |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Grade-A Steel (Pure/Dvergr-forged) | 0.1mm/hour | Resistant |
| Grade-B Steel (Standard) | 2mm/hour | Catastrophic for unprotected gear |
| Grade-C Steel (Scrap-quality) | 5mm/hour | Structural failure within 4 hours |
| Organic Materials (leather, wood) | Immediate dissolution | Total vulnerability |

### Toxicity Assessment

| Exposure Route | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Inhalation (vapor)** | Type Beta CPS onset within 10 minutes (confusion, disorientation) |
| **Skin Contact** | Chemical burns (2nd degree) within 30 seconds |
| **Ingestion** | Fatal within 2 hours (organ failure cascade) |
| **Chronic Exposure** | Rusting Lung syndrome (metal particulate accumulation) |

### Decontamination Protocol

1. **Immediate:** Dilute alkali wash (sodium bicarbonate solution, 10% concentration)
2. **Secondary:** Fresh water rinse (minimum 5 minutes continuous flow)
3. **Tertiary:** Medical assessment for chemical burns and CPS symptoms
4. **Equipment:** Discard Grade-C gear; deep-clean Grade-B gear; Grade-A reusable after decon

### Safe Handling Protocols

- **Minimum PPE:** Full-body sealed suit (Grade-B minimum), respirator with acid-gas filters
- **Exposure Limits:** ≤15 minutes at chasm edge, ≤5 minutes direct sludge handling
- **Transport:** Sealed glass containers only (metal containers corrode within 1 hour)
- **Storage:** Acid-resistant cabinets with ventilation; maximum 7-day storage (compound instability)
