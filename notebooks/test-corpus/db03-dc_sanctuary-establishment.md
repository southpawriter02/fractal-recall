# 🏰 Sanctuary Establishment Protocol — Early Hold Consolidation (~1-10 PG)

## Observation Summary

**Observer:** Midgard Combine Registry (Institutional compilation)
**Timeframe:** Years 1-10 PG (Age of Silence, immediate post-Glitch period)
**Geographic Scope:** Central Midgard settlements
**Evidence Tier:** B (Secondary Source — retrospective compilation from fragmentary records)

---

## Context

This data capture documents the earliest systematic sanctuary establishment protocols during Years 1-10 PG, when survivor enclaves consolidated into fortified Holds. Documentation compiled retrospectively (Years 20-50 PG) from surviving administrative records, oral testimonies, and Rangers Guild logs.

**Key Constraint:** Record-keeping was tertiary to survival during this period. Many entries are incomplete, dates approximate, and protocols inferred from outcomes rather than direct observation.

---

## Sanctuary Establishment Protocol (Field Report Format)

**Threshold Conditions:**
- Survivor population: 50-200 individuals (minimum viable)
- Salvageable materials within 1-2 day travel radius
- Defensible terrain (elevated ground, natural barriers, or ruins)
- Potable water source within settlement perimeter
- Arable land potential (Ash-Fall Earth, minimal Blight contamination)

**Decision Authority:** Early Jarls (emerged organically from survivor group leadership)

**Establishment Sequence (Observed Pattern):**

### Phase 1: Site Selection (Weeks 1-4)

**Observable Actions:**
- Rangers or scouts survey 3-5 potential sites
- Jarl convenes council of senior survivors
- Site selected based on defensibility and resource proximity
- Perimeter markers established (cairns, salvaged material boundaries)

**Observed Criteria:**
- Distance from known Blight-Storm epicenters
- Proximity to ruins (salvage material source)
- Natural defensive advantages (cliffs, ravines, ruins)
- Water source reliability

### Phase 2: Perimeter Defense (Weeks 4-12)

**Observable Actions:**
- Initial wall construction (salvaged ferrocrete, scrap metal, petrified ironwood)
- Wall height: 2-4 meters (varied by available materials)
- Watch posts established at 4 cardinal points
- Rangers establish patrol routes connecting to other nascent settlements

**Observed Pattern:**
> "The Wall is the soul of the Hold" — cultural priority observed across all documented sanctuaries. Wall construction preceded all other infrastructure.

### Phase 3: Resource Infrastructure (Weeks 12-52)

**Observable Actions:**
- Grain storage pits or salvaged silos repaired
- Communal shelters constructed (materials: salvaged habitat modules, ruins, timber)
- Hearth-fires established (charcoal production, metalworking)
- Latrine trenches dug at perimeter edge

**Observed Variation:**
- Successful sanctuaries: Grain stores established within 6 months
- Failed sanctuaries: Resource infrastructure delayed past first harvest → starvation

### Phase 4: Governance Formalization (Year 1-2)

**Observable Actions:**
- Jarl authority codified (oral tradition, witnessed oaths)
- Hold council formed (Grain Master, Wall Captain, Healer, Rangers liaison)
- Inter-Hold agreements established (trade routes, mutual defense)
- Rangers Guild emerges as connective tissue between isolated Holds

**Observed Challenge:**
> "Who guards the guardians?" — early disputes over Jarl succession documented in 30% of Holds. Formalized succession protocols emerged by Year 10 PG.

## Quantified Observations

**Sanctuary Success Rates (Years 1-10 PG):**
- Documented sanctuary attempts: ~200+ (fragmentary records)
- Survived to Year 10 PG: ~80-100 (40-50% success rate)
- Primary failure causes: Starvation (40%), Blight-Spawn incursion (30%), internal conflict (15%), disease (15%)

**Timeline Patterns:**
- Wall construction: 2-3 months (minimum viable defense)
- Full fortification: 1-2 years (stone reinforcement, gate mechanisms)
- Grain self-sufficiency: 6-12 months (first successful harvest)
- Population stabilization: 2-5 years (births exceed deaths)

**Resource Requirements (Observed Averages):**
- Initial population: 50-200 individuals
- Salvageable material radius: 5-10 kilometers (2-day foraging range)
- Arable land per capita: ~0.5 hectares (Ash-Fall Earth, low yield)
- Daily grain ration: ~500 grams per adult (survival minimum)

## Observer Limitations

**Source Reliability Constraints:**

1. **Retrospective Compilation:** Most records created 20-50 years after events. Dates approximate, protocols inferred.
2. **Survivor Bias:** Only successful sanctuaries left records. Failed settlements underrepresented.
3. **Geographic Bias:** Central Midgard Holds over-represented. Frontier or isolated sanctuaries poorly documented.
4. **Oral Tradition Variance:** Jarl testimonies collected decades later. Cultural hero-narratives may distort operational reality.
5. **Administrative Focus:** Registry prioritizes logistics (grain stores, wall height, population) over cultural practices or personal narratives.
