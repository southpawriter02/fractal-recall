## Diagnostic Summary

This assembled entry catalogues the major medical phenomena, pathological conditions, and physiological adaptations documented across the post-Glitch realms. The Ginnungagap Glitch and its aftermath produced an unprecedented range of pathologies — from ambient Aetheric field contamination to radical physiological adaptation. Understanding these conditions is essential for field medical personnel, sanctuary administrators, and population health planners.

The catalogue organises conditions by primary mechanism: Aetheric exposure pathologies, environmental adaptation syndromes, and anomalous physiological states.

---

## §1 — Aetheric Exposure Pathologies

### Cognitive Paradox Syndrome (CPS)

The most common Aetheric pathology. Acute onset following exposure to concentrated Runic Blight radiation. Symptoms: rapid cognitive deterioration, paradoxical ideation (inability to distinguish contradictory statements), sensory hallucination, and progressive personality dissolution. Severity correlates with exposure intensity and duration.

**Incidence:** 12-18% of populations regularly operating in Blight-adjacent zones. Universal across all realms.

**Prognosis:** Mild cases (Grade 1-2) recover with distance from exposure source. Moderate cases (Grade 3) stabilise but retain permanent cognitive deficits. Severe cases (Grade 4-5) are terminal; median survival 3-8 months post-onset.

**Treatment:** Removal from exposure environment. Dvergr-crafted resonance dampening equipment. Cognitive rehabilitation protocols. No curative treatment exists.

### Harrow-Sick

A progressive degenerative condition distinct from acute CPS. Develops over months to years through chronic low-level Aetheric exposure. Characterised by three stages: The Dimming (onset), The Harrowing (progression), and The Hollowing (terminal). Produces subcutaneous iron deposits, cognitive fragmentation, and eventual multi-system organ failure.

**Incidence:** 3-7% of frontier populations; 15-25% of salvage workers.

**Prognosis:** Stage 1 shows ~40% stabilisation rate with immediate exposure cessation. Stage 2 and beyond: no documented recovery. Terminal within 18-36 months from Stage 2 onset.

**Treatment:** Stage 1 — exposure cessation, resonance dampening, herbal palliatives. Stage 2 — iron deposit management, cognitive support. Stage 3 — palliative care only. See dedicated Harrow-Sick codex entry for comprehensive documentation.

### Blight-Burn

Acute tissue damage from direct contact with concentrated Aetheric discharge (Blight-Storm debris, active runic malfunction, or weaponised Aetheric pulse). Presents as characteristic fractal-pattern burns that resist conventional wound healing. Scarring follows Aetheric field line geometry rather than anatomical structure.

**Incidence:** Episodic; associated with Blight-Storm events and industrial runic accidents.

**Prognosis:** Surface burns heal within 4-8 weeks with specialised treatment. Deep burns may produce permanent functional impairment. Blight-Burn scarring is permanent and cosmetically distinctive.

**Treatment:** Dvergr wound salves with Aetheric dampening compounds. Standard burn protocols otherwise applicable. Avoid conventional metal surgical instruments on active burn sites (metal conducts residual Aetheric charge).

## §2 — Environmental Adaptation Syndromes

### Depth Sickness

Neurological syndrome affecting individuals transitioning rapidly between surface environments and deep strata (Nidavellir mining zones, Svartalfheim approaches). Symptoms: vertigo, tinnitus, spatial disorientation, claustrophobic panic, and temporary proprioceptive failure. Mechanism: rapid change in ambient Aetheric field density between surface and deep environments.

**Incidence:** 8-12% of surface-origin personnel deployed to deep strata operations.

**Prognosis:** Self-limiting in most cases. Symptoms resolve within 48-72 hours with acclimatisation. Chronic Depth Sickness (persistent symptoms beyond 2 weeks) affects ~2% of cases and may require permanent surface reassignment.

**Treatment:** Gradual depth acclimatisation protocols. Resonance dampening during transition periods. Dvergr herbal preparations (specifically Blind-Root tea) for symptom relief.

### Cold Adaptation Syndrome (Niflheim populations)

Metabolic shifts observed in long-term Niflheim residents (particularly Forsaken populations). Reduced peripheral circulation, enhanced brown fat deposits, altered thyroid function, and modified pain perception threshold. These adaptations improve survival in extreme cold but produce vulnerability to warm-environment exposure.

**Incidence:** Near-universal in Niflheim populations resident >5 years.

**Treatment:** Generally not treated as pathology — represents successful environmental adaptation. Medical intervention required only when individuals relocate to temperate environments and experience thermoregulatory instability.

## §3 — Anomalous Physiological States

### Void-Marked Condition

Stable Aetheric symbiosis resulting from acute Blight exposure. Produces permanent physical markers (dermal Void-Lines, ocular transformation), altered Aetheric perception, and complete immunity to Harrow-Sick. Affects approximately 1 in 50 individuals experiencing significant acute Blight exposure.

**Classification debate:** Whether the Void-Marked condition constitutes pathology, adaptation, or a distinct physiological state remains contested. Jötun-Reader Medical Division classifies it as "Anomalous Stable Integration" — deliberately neutral terminology avoiding both the pathological framing preferred by Iron-Bane doctrine and the quasi-sacred framing advanced by some Void-Marked advocacy groups.

### Post-Human Divergence Syndromes

Documented in populations meeting Silent Folk, Ljósálfar, and Dökkálfar diagnostic criteria. These represent radical, heritable physiological transformations rather than reversible conditions. See dedicated species entries for clinical specification.

## §4 — Diagnostic Infrastructure and Limitations

**Jötun-Reader Medical Division** maintains the primary case registry and diagnostic methodology for all catalogued conditions. Standardised assessment protocols exist for CPS grading, Harrow-Sick staging, and Void-Marked identification.

**Limitations:**

- No pre-Glitch medical baseline data survives for comparison
- Diagnostic instrumentation is limited to Dvergr resonance measurement and observational assessment
- Longitudinal studies constrained by population mobility and factional access restrictions
- Ethical constraints prohibit controlled exposure studies
- Sample sizes for rare conditions (Void-Marked conversion events) insufficient for statistical analysis
