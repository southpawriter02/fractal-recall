## Summary

**Pre-Glitch Function:** Project Gungnir was the Aesir-Vanir research initiative to develop the All-Rune — a universal runic compiler intended to harmonize all contradictory rune functions across the Yggdrasil Network.

**Research Scope:** Theoretical & Applied Aetheric Research (Deck 05) dedicated years to developing syntax that could resolve paradoxes in runic substrate. Ginnungagap Labs served as primary testing facility.

**Catastrophic Failure:** All-Rune compilation triggered recursive paradox execution during Ginnungagap Glitch. "The All-Rune cannot compile" became perpetual ghost process creating Runic Blight.

**Counter-Rune Connection:** Jörmungandr Counter-Rune was developed as failsafe/stabilization mechanism for All-Rune. Dual nature (parasitic AND world-ending) represents core Apocrypha mystery.

**Post-Glitch Legacy:** Ginnungagap Labs in orbital Asgard contain All-Rune forge site with residual Dagaz arrays. Heimdallr Signal broadcasts permanently looping All-Rune paradox echo.

## I. Project Objectives and Development Timeline

### 1.1 Strategic Motivation

Project Gungnir emerged from fundamental Aesir philosophy: **the belief that a unified logical framework could harmonize all contradictory elements in the Yggdrasil Network.**

**Core Objective:**
Develop a single, universal runic syntax capable of resolving contradictions between different rune functions. The Aesir recognized that individual runes often contained paradoxical directives—Berkano's growth principle conflicted with Thurisaz's containment principle; Dagaz's dawning opposed Hagalaz's disruption.

**Strategic Hypothesis:**
A sufficiently sophisticated compiler (the All-Rune) could resolve these paradoxes through meta-level abstraction: a command that simultaneously satisfied both growth AND containment through contextual reframing.

**Technical Design:**
