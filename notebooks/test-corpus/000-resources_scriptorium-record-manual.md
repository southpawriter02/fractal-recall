## 1.0 Scope and Definitions

The Record Manual governs how Readers turn unstable events into durable knowledge. Its scope is network‑wide and binding wherever the Creed's seal is recognized. In practice, that means every Scribe who carries a slate, every Archivist who tends a vault, every Lex‑Guardian who escorts a courier through a crowd, and every Rúnasmiðr who posts a rollback path at the bench operates under this manual's authority. Exceptions exist, but only by ink: a written countersign from the appropriate council with reasons, timebox, and rollback stated plainly.

"Records" in this manual are not abstractions. They are physical things and their validated derivatives: field logs written in a clear hand, incident ledgers with page‑notch confirmations, forge ledgers that tie a line of craft to a specific vein and lot, Codex submissions with provenance trails, and public notes at Common Clearance meant to steady frightened neighbors without promising what cannot be promised.

Integrity terms appear throughout and must be read as instruments, not ornaments. A "checksum phrase" is a spoken and recorded guard against drift, exchanged aloud at handoff so the body remembers what the ink says. "Dual‑entry custody" means two ledgers move in parallel—unit and node—so that a single loss cannot erase the truth. A "mirrored transcript" is a couriered second copy whose agreement with the first is checked on receipt; any divergence triggers quarantine and inquiry. "Countersign" is not flourish, but a second mind accepting the burden.

This manual assumes Layer 2 voice throughout. Field teams record myth as heard, then translate it in the operational copy with terms that hold under repeat observation. Where a perfect equivalent does not exist, we prefer an honest analog accompanied by an uncertainty note to any show of precision we cannot defend. The measure of our writing is not cleverness, but whether another crew, on another day, could repeat the observation and arrive at the same ledger line.

## 2.0 Authoring Protocols (Layer Discipline)

Authoring is an instrument, not a performance. The purpose of style in this manual is to preserve meaning across stations, seasons, and stress. Every sentence should help another Reader reproduce the observation and reach the same ledger line. What follows converts the outline into practice that can be taught to a tired crew at a cold table and still hold.

### 2.1 Voice: Record What Is

Write in the Layer 2 clinical voice. Describe phenomena as measured, not as hoped or feared. When witnesses speak in Layer 1 terms, record them verbatim in the witness transcript, then translate in the operational copy. Do not editorialize the person. Translate the nouns.

- If no precise Layer 2 term exists, supply the closest defensible analog and add an uncertainty note. Do not invent consensus where none exists.
- Prefer short, declarative sentences over flourishes. The test for keeping a sentence is whether it makes a measurement or a decision clearer.

### 2.2 Structure: Make It Reproducible

Follow the World Bible Resource Template: header block, decimal outline, and first‑mention links to canonical v4.0 resources. Each numbered subsection should advance one idea from observation to decision with no hidden steps.

- Decimal hierarchy: Use 2.0, 2.1, 2.1.1. Do not skip levels. If a subsection cannot stand alone when excerpted, it is not finished.
- Checklists in SOP sections must be rendered as "Before / During / After" so field teams can speak them aloud. If a step cannot be spoken in one breath, split it.

### 2.3 Identifiers and Versioning: Sync the Numbers

Document IDs follow AAM-\\<DOMAIN\\>-\\<SUBJECT\\>-vX.Y and must match the visible header and the database Version property. Minor bumps record editorial clarity or non‑substantive adjustments. Major bumps denote real changes to procedures, thresholds, or definitions. Every bump includes a one‑line "Revision Note" stating why the version moved and what changed.

## 3.0 Custody and Provenance (Chain of Trust)

Custody is how we keep a fact alive when memory, pressure, or malice try to change it. Provenance is the map that shows where the fact came from and who touched it. Together they turn a page into testimony. This section defines the chain, the instruments that protect it, and the actions to take when it bends or breaks.

### 3.1 Field Creation: Start the Chain Aloud

Before the first line is written, the Recorder speaks date, time, role, and location. The Timekeeper marks exposure ceilings in the margin and keeps the cadence. If witnesses are present, record their words verbatim in the witness transcript; do not correct their speech in the moment.

- Spoken header (required): date, time, role, location, checksum phrase.
- Instruments: slate, wax, page‑notch awl, sealed ink, sand‑timer.
- Abort note: If ceiling ticks out mid‑entry, stop where you are. Seal the page; add the remainder in a continuation entry in a cold zone.

Example spoken header: "On the 14th of Harvest, at second bell after sundown, Recorder Ilda, Tertius‑12, Midgard Deck East Gate. Checksum phrase: Dagaz Nine."

### 3.2 Dual‑Entry Ledger: Two Books or It Didn't Happen

Every custody event is written twice—once in the unit ledger, once in the node ledger. A handoff is not complete until both ledgers carry matching entries, two signatures, and the checksum phrase spoken by both parties. Page‑notch confirmation and seal inspection are recorded at handoff.

- Required fields: source, destination, items, seal state, notch pattern, checksum phrase, signatures.
- Signatures: the giver and the receiver. A third countersign is added where the protocol requires (e.g., duty clerk or council representative).
- Checksum phrases rotate by node and month; treat them as keys, not slogans.

### 3.3 Mirrored Transcripts: Redundancy Against Loss

A couriered second copy is created for each primary record. On receipt at the node, the clerk reads the checksum phrase aloud, compares page‑notches and content, and marks "agree" or "diverge." Any divergence, however minor, triggers quarantine of both copies and an inquiry note opened within 24 hours.

- Agreement states: Agree, Diverge‑minor (spelling, spacing), Diverge‑material (content or count).
- Action on Diverge‑material: quarantine both, notify Protocol Desk, assign inquiry owner, and log timeline.
- Courier risks: If a courier is delayed past safe window, log delay and reason; do not compress checks to "catch up."

### 3.4 Ingest and Quarantine: From Doorway to Vault

On arrival at Primus or an authorized node, all new records enter quarantine shelving for provenance audit before vault placement. Intake includes seal inspection, notch check, inventory of enclosures, and a quick baseline scan for contamination where applicable. Do not read raw hazard logs in quarantine; follow the Codex safe‑view rubric.

- Intake steps (Before / During / After):
  - Before: Prepare shelf, gloves, tools; announce intake; set timer.
  - During: Inspect seal and notches; tally contents; speak checksum phrase; record variances.
  - After: Place in quarantine; file intake note; schedule provenance audit within 48 hours.

### 3.5 Minimum Provenance Dossier: What Makes a Truth Canon‑Ready

No record advances to canon without a minimum dossier sufficient for another team to retrace steps. The dossier binds evidence to method and people.

- Contents list:
  - Witness transcript and recorder's operational copy
  - Instrument list and environmental baselines
  - Sketches or rubbings as applicable
  - Custody chain entries (unit and node) with signatures and checksum phrases
  - Mirrored transcript receipt with agree/diverge status
  - Any quarantine notes and resolutions

## 4.0 The Fourth Canon — The Creedal Vow (Neutrality and Scope)

The Creed's sovereignty is over methods, not territories. Our oath binds us to legibility above victory. This section turns that vow into working limits: whom we serve, how we govern ourselves, and what we refuse no matter the pressure.

### 4.1 Neutrality Pact — Who We Serve

Neutrality is not silence and not surrender. It is disciplined speech confined to observation, method, and consequence. When others demand banners, we publish measurements. When demanded for judgments, we decline and cite scope.

- Prime loyalty: Truth preserved and made legible. Not Jarls, caravans, or cults.
- Public interface: Use Common Clearance notes to inform without prescribing action. Describe hazards, windows, and geometry; omit endorsements and blame.
- Refusals: Decline requests for affidavits that would function as war banners. Offer a public note instead with measured facts and exposure ceilings.

### 4.2 Governance — Councils Over Thrones

Authority is distributed to prevent charisma from overruling craft. Decisions are scoped, timeboxed, and reviewable; irreversible acts require countersigns.

- Authority stack:
  - Archivist‑Prime: Chairs the Inner Circle, stewards coherence, cannot overrule Ethics on irreversible harm.
  - Inner Circle councils: Ethics, Codex, Signal‑Taming, Rúnasmiðr Engineering, Field Ops. Vote inside remit; publish minutes with redactions.
  - Local command: Archivist‑Magister or Incident Commander on scene within guardrails; ties escalate to duty officer on a clock.
- Review cadence: AAR within 72 hours for Class‑Beta+ events; quarterly Standards Board to ratify or revoke provisional protocols.

## 5.0 The Fifth Canon — Ethical Calculus of the Great Work

Curiosity without brakes is cruelty with good manners. The Ethical Calculus names the brakes: who can say no, when harm outweighs yield, and how we count the cost so it is never hidden in poetry or pride. This section operationalizes consent, veto power, aftercare, and audit so knowledge is rescued without worshiping suffering.

### 5.1 Hollow Scholars — Patients, Not Projects

Terminal CPS subjects are maintained under non‑intervention doctrine with burden‑of‑care. They are living instruments only insofar as their continued life permits observation without additional harm.

- Standing orders: Daily ethics rounds and Keeper countersign. Publish clearance‑appropriate public notes that report non‑identifiable metrics when safety requires community trust.
- Veto: The Ethics Officer holds a standing, immediate veto on any act that increases suffering without diagnostic yield. No appeal in the moment; arguments occur after, on paper.
- Privacy: External study uses anonymized case IDs; raw streams remain quarantined to the Keeper's console.

### 5.2 Echo‑Harvest — Last‑Resort Protocol

Extracting data from agony is not routine work; it is a gated exception with high burden. Preconditions must be satisfied in full before any exposure.

- Preconditions: Informed consent where capacity remains or prior consent doctrine where culturally recognized; Lex‑Guardian overwatch; stated exposure ceilings; aftercare scheduled.
- Handling: Capture analyst‑safe spectra only for circulation; raw buffers remain quarantined. No public skaldry from a scream.
- Accounting: Each Echo‑Harvest includes a moral addendum stating why expected yield outweighed harm risked or endured, signed by Ethics and domain lead.

### 5.3 Failure as Data — Against Shame and Drift

We archive errors because shame breeds secrecy and secrecy breeds drift. A failure hidden will repeat; a failure taught may save a life.

- AAR window: Class‑Beta and above incidents require after‑action within 72 hours; include ethics addendum when CPS exposure occurred.
- Red‑team: Quarterly adversarial reviews alternate between Ethics and Signal‑Taming to challenge assumptions around Silent Rooms, Echo‑Harvest, and firewall trials.
- Publication: Minutes are published with redactions as needed; corrective actions receive owners and dates.

## 6.0 The Sixth Canon — Containment by Craft (The Rúnasmiðr Charge)

Containment is not purification. A runic firewall is a physical, ritual inscription that carves out a sterile pocket in a hostile medium; it does not cleanse the world, it buys time for thinking. This section turns craft into procedure so pockets are predictable, perishable, and safe to abandon.

### 6.1 Materials Discipline — Inert or Nothing

Firewalls fail first in the material. A beautiful impurity is still a flaw; reject it.

- Substrates: Certified inert crystal lots and true steel from trusted veins only. Any hint of paradox‑memory signature rejects the batch.
- Tools and inks: Scribing awls, gauge blocks, non‑reactive inks. Salvaged alloys are never carriers; at most, they are clamps outside the ward edge.
- Ledgering: Lot numbers, vein of origin, inspector marks recorded on the release note and echoed in the forge ledger.

### 6.2 Firewall as Artifact — What It Is, What It Isn't

Define the thing to prevent superstition from filling gaps.

- Definition: A "runic firewall" is a specific geometry and ritual on inert media that dampens or deflects paradox influence, creating a sterile pocket. It is not programmable, not updatable in place, and always perishable.
- Behavior: Each use is a new artifact with unique failure modes. Treat time, heat, and repetition as decay vectors.

### 6.3 Trial Sequence — Bench → Tent → Field

Trials pace risk and require a rollback path in writing before any live exposure.

- Bench slate: Inscribe on small inert tiles. Observe for inversion precursors under controlled temperature and noise.
- Sterile‑tent mock: Scale geometry under canvas. Repeat measures with timeboxed exposure and Lex‑Guardian overwatch.
- Staged field pilot: Deploy at standoff with pre‑posted rollback steps. Abort on any precursor. Countersigned release note is filed with the forge ledger.

## 7.0 The Seventh Canon — Signal and Cognitive Hazard Handling

Signals and entities are both sources and weapons. Curiosity must be choreographed so that we acquire without inviting inversion, and publish without unmaking the reader. This section defines capture choreography, sanitization, abort logic, and dissemination tiers.

### 7.1 Acquisition — How to Touch the Fire Without Catching

Acquire at distance first, under shields, with fixed sequences that scrub dangerous harmonics before human review.

- Cages and trunks: Hand‑crank Faraday cages, braided‑mesh signal trunks, grounded reels only. No naked wire in hot or warm zones.
- Shutter sequences: Run approved shutter orders. If any setting induces resonance (audible or felt), halt and fall back to the prior setting. Record time index and conditions.
- Distance discipline: Prefer remote capture posts at standoff using mirrors and acoustic relays. Close approach occurs only after a clean remote pass.

### 7.2 Handling and Sanitization — What May Be Seen

Raw buffers are weapons until proven otherwise. All handling routes through quarantine and scrubbing before analysts read.

- Quarantine: Raw reels reside at the Keeper's console only; no export or duplication outside the cage.
- Multi‑pass scrubbing: Apply phase‑scrub pipelines to remove paradox harmonics; document passes and residuals.
- Analyst‑safe spectra: Only export sanitized spectra or transcripts that pass the Codex safe‑view rubric. Every export is stamped with a checksum phrase and countersigned in the ledger.

### 7.3 Abort Conditions — The Black‑Flag Without Shame

Aborts are not failures; they are the procedure working. Define triggers, speak them, and act without debate when they appear.

- Triggers: Paradox spike on meter. Repeating phrase‑locks in personnel. Self‑directed approach by an entity. Inversion cues in firewall telemetry.
- Action: Drop the black‑flag. Pull tension‑wires. Retreat by drill lanes to cold zone. No appeals in hot. Log trigger, time, distance.

## 8.0 The Eighth Canon — Governance and Accountability

Power accrues to evidence, not charisma. Governance exists to keep methods legible under stress and to ensure that every irreversible act leaves a paper body behind it. This section defines authority boundaries, review cadences, breach regimes, and how the work judges itself in public.

### 8.1 Authority Stack — Who Decides What

Authority is scoped to prevent ambition from masquerading as doctrine. No role owns outcomes; roles own procedures.

- Archivist‑Prime: First among peers, steward of coherence, chairs the Inner Circle. Cannot overrule Ethics on irreversible harm. Signals cadence for doctrine updates.
- Inner Circle Councils: Ethics, Codex, Signal‑Taming, Rúnasmiðr Engineering, Field Ops. Vote within remit; minutes published with redactions.
- Local Command: Archivist‑Magister or Incident Commander governs scenes within guardrails; tie‑breaks escalate to duty officer on a clock.

### 8.2 Review Cadence — How the Work Judges Itself

Reviews create friction where drift begins. The cadence is designed to surface disagreement before it becomes dogma.

- AAR Window: Class‑Beta+ incidents require an After‑Action Review within 72 hours including timeline, thresholds crossed, and proposed fixes.
- Standards Board: Quarterly reconvening to ratify or revoke provisional protocols; publish minutes and assign owners with dates.
- Red‑Team Cycle: Alternating adversarial audits by Ethics and Signal‑Taming challenge assumptions and detect ritual drift.

### 8.3 Breach Regime — Lines, Consequences, Records

A breach is recorded, not whispered. Mercy is documented; so is removal.

- Negligence: Retraining with remediation plan and follow‑up audit in a season.
- Willful Violation: Immediate removal from post, sealed record in the Loki Archive, corridor notice at appropriate clearance explaining breach category.
- Mercy Clause: Where procedure failed a person, not the reverse, the Board may attach a clemency appendix to the ledger.

## 9.0 The Heresy Canon (Restricted) — On Loki's Counter‑Logic

This canon is restricted. It exists because the Jörmungandr Counter‑Rune is the only logic observed to co‑exist with the Blight without immediate inversion. Curiosity here is a knife without a handle. Study proceeds only under sealed procedure, on a clock, with two minds present, and with a rollback path inked before any contact.

### 9.1 Scope and Access — Inner Only

- Clearance: Inner Circle only. No apprentices, no guests. Keys rotate; entry requires two persons present at the vault door and inside the room.
- Materials: Raw‑adjacent notes stored in sealed drawers; no removal from cage. Any derivative copy is stamped, countersigned, and logged against the drawer ID.

### 9.2 Controls — Brakes First, Curiosity Second

- Two‑person rules: No solo work. Ethics and Codex maintain alternating watch. Either may halt proceedings immediately.
- Timers and windows: Work only within defined attenuation windows. Speak start and stop aloud; wax‑seal ledger at stop.
- Rollback path: Pre‑written, posted at bench, and rehearsed before first contact. Includes purge steps for buffers, reseal steps for drawers, and corridor messaging for abort.

### 9.3 Method — How to Look Without Becoming

- Approach: Treat counter‑logic like corrosive. Use inert carriers and intermediary inscriptions; never apply directly to standing firewalls.
- Instrumentation: Separate counters from sensors with inert gaps. No closed loops; interpose human read and re‑write to break feedback chains.
- Documentation: Record verbatim glyph orders and observed effects, then translate into Layer 2 description. Note uncertainty openly; do not "complete" patterns to satisfy symmetry.

### 9.4 Ethics Gate — The Line Between Cure and Contagion

- Burden statement: Before work, state expected yield and who bears the burden if harm manifests. Ethics veto cannot be appealed in the moment.
- Publication: Only analyst‑safe abstracts leave the cage; do not arm skalds with dangerous poetry.
