# Medical Causality Research — Aetheric Pathology and the Void-Marked Spectrum

## Observation Context

**Compiled by:** Jötun-Reader Medical Division, Causal Pathology Research Unit
**Research period:** Ongoing, primary data collection Year 600-783 PG
**Methodology:** Case-control analysis, longitudinal cohort tracking, post-mortem tissue analysis, Dvergr resonance measurement
**Purpose:** To establish causal mechanisms linking Aetheric Blight exposure to divergent medical outcomes — specifically, why identical exposure conditions produce Harrow-Sick in some individuals and Void-Marked status in others

---

## §1 — Research Premise and Scope

The central question of post-Glitch medical causality research is deceptively simple: **why does the same environmental exposure produce opposite outcomes in different individuals?**

Aetheric Blight exposure at clinically significant levels produces three documented outcomes:

1. **Cognitive Paradox Syndrome (CPS):** Acute neurological damage — most common outcome (~80% of significant exposures)
2. **Harrow-Sick:** Chronic degenerative pathology — moderate incidence (~18% of significant exposures)
3. **Void-Marked Condition:** Stable Aetheric integration — rare (~2% of significant exposures)

The divergence between outcomes 2 and 3 — chronic degeneration versus stable adaptation — represents the most important unsolved question in post-Glitch medicine. This research programme documents the current state of causal mechanism investigation.

---

## §2 — Harrow-Sick Etiology — Current Evidence

### 2.1 — Established Causal Factors

**Exposure chronicity:** Harrow-Sick development correlates strongly with exposure duration rather than intensity. Brief acute exposure (Blight-Storm passage, runic malfunction) produces CPS, not Harrow-Sick. Extended low-level exposure (months of salvage work in Blight-adjacent zones) produces Harrow-Sick progression.

**Tissue-level mechanism:** Post-mortem tissue analysis (n=247 confirmed Harrow-Sick cases) demonstrates corrupted Aetheric particle accumulation in metabolically active tissues — liver, kidney, neural tissue, and bone marrow. Particle density follows a gradient pattern consistent with passive diffusion from the external environment rather than active uptake.

**Iron deposit formation:** The characteristic subcutaneous iron deposits form through a biomineralisation process in which the body sequesters corrupted Aetheric particles within iron-rich crystalline matrices. This appears to be a defensive response — containing contamination — that ultimately fails as deposit volume exceeds tissue tolerance and begins generating localised Aetheric field distortions.

### 2.2 — Proposed Causal Models

**Model A — Threshold Exhaustion:** Each individual possesses a finite cellular repair capacity for Aetheric damage. Chronic exposure gradually exhausts this capacity; once repair mechanisms are overwhelmed, progressive degeneration begins. The model predicts that all individuals would eventually develop Harrow-Sick given sufficient exposure — the Void-Marked simply represent an alternative pathway activated before threshold exhaustion.

**Model B — Inflammatory Cascade:** Corrupted Aetheric particles trigger an auto-immune-analogous inflammatory response. In Harrow-Sick patients, this response becomes self-sustaining: inflammation generates tissue damage, which exposes more cells to Aetheric contamination, which triggers further inflammation. The iron deposits represent collateral damage of the ongoing inflammatory process.

**Model C — Genetic Susceptibility:** Harrow-Sick development is determined by heritable genetic variants affecting Aetheric particle processing at the cellular level. This model requires identifying specific genetic markers — a task complicated by the absence of pre-Glitch genetic databases and the limited resolution of post-Glitch diagnostic technology.

## §3 — Void-Marked Divergence — The Core Mystery

### 3.1 — Observed Divergence Points

Analysis of documented "conversion events" — cases where early-stage Harrow-Sick symptoms resolve and Void-Marked markers appear — provides the strongest evidence for understanding the divergence mechanism:

**Timing:** Conversion events occur exclusively during the transition from Stage 1 to Stage 2 Harrow-Sick. No conversions documented from Stage 2 or beyond. This suggests a critical decision window during which the body's response to Aetheric contamination can redirect from degenerative rejection to stable integration.

**Physiological markers:** During conversion events, patients exhibit:

- Sudden fever (39-41°C) lasting 24-48 hours
- Cessation of all Harrow-Sick symptoms within 72 hours of fever resolution
- Appearance of Void-Line dermal patterns within 7-14 days
- Stabilisation of Aetheric resonance signatures within 30 days

**Frequency:** Approximately 1 in 200 Stage 1 Harrow-Sick patients undergo spontaneous conversion. No reliable predictors identified.

### 3.2 — Proposed Divergence Models

**Adaptation Model:** The Void-Marked body develops novel cellular machinery — biological analogues to Aetheric processing systems — that incorporate corrupted particles as metabolic substrates rather than toxic contaminants. The conversion fever represents the metabolic cost of constructing this new cellular architecture.

**Pre-adaptation Model:** Genetic variants present before exposure predetermine outcome. Some individuals possess latent Aetheric integration capacity — perhaps a remnant of pre-Glitch bio-engineering — that activates under exposure stress. The Harrow-Sick/Void-Marked divergence is genetically determined; environmental exposure merely triggers expression.

**Threshold Bifurcation Model:** A unified model proposing that all individuals follow the same initial response pathway. At a critical Aetheric saturation threshold, the body's response bifurcates: cellular repair systems either collapse (Harrow-Sick) or reorganise (Void-Marked). The determining factor may be the specific Aetheric particle profile encountered — subtly different Blight compositions producing different cellular responses.

## §4 — Unsolved Questions and Research Barriers

### 4.1 — Priority Questions

1. **Genetic determination:** Can Harrow-Sick susceptibility or Void-Marked potential be predicted before exposure? If so, pre-screening of at-risk populations becomes possible
2. **Conversion trigger:** What specifically initiates the Harrow-Sick to Void-Marked conversion event? Can it be induced therapeutically in early-stage Harrow-Sick patients?
3. **Weir-Bone interaction:** Why does Weir-Bone proximity enhance Void-Marked perception but show no effect on Harrow-Sick progression? Does the material interact with the same biological pathways that determine divergence outcome?
4. **Long-term Void-Marked stability:** Is the Void-Marked condition permanently stable, or could late-onset degeneration emerge? The oldest confirmed Void-Marked is 74 years post-exposure — insufficient for definitive long-term prognosis

### 4.2 — Research Barriers

**Ethical constraints:** The most significant barrier. Deliberate Blight exposure studies are prohibited under Jötun-Reader ethical guidelines. All research relies on retrospective analysis of natural exposure events — introducing uncontrollable variables (exposure composition, duration, individual health status) that complicate causal inference.

**Diagnostic technology limitations:** Dvergr resonance measurement provides functional Aetheric field data but cannot resolve cellular-level processes. Tissue analysis requires post-mortem specimens — unavailable for the Void-Marked condition (living subjects, no biopsy protocol validated).

**Political interference:** Iron-Bane doctrine classifies Void-Marked research as "Blight collaboration." Research teams operating in Crusade-adjacent territories have been harassed, and inter-factional data sharing is complicated by Iron-Bane embargo demands.

**Population access:** Void-Marked populations are dispersed and socially marginalised. Systematic cohort recruitment requires Warden-Host cooperation — available but logistically challenging across seventeen sanctuary sites.

## Evidence Justification

**Tier B — Strong circumstantial evidence** with quantitative support. Case-control methodology provides statistically meaningful comparisons (n=247 Harrow-Sick, n=89 Void-Marked, n=1,200 CPS controls). Longitudinal cohort data available for subsets. Post-mortem tissue analysis provides cellular-level evidence for Harrow-Sick mechanism. Void-Marked mechanism remains hypothetical — no direct experimental confirmation of any proposed model.
